# Core Papers

The `core/` directory contains the theoretical spine of Geometric Reflexive
Coherence. These papers trace the movement from the original Reflexive Organism
Model (ROM) to the coherence-only field formulation, then to identity, agency,
abundance, multiscale/fractal extension, local continuation geometry, and
read-back.

Read together, they answer one question:

```text
What kind of system can define itself, preserve identity, generate its own
geometry, experience choice locally, and remain coherent across scale?
```

## Paper Summaries

### [Reflexive Organism Model](2025-08-ReflexiveOrganismModel.md)

This is the originating paper. It begins from biological and ecological
observations, especially superorganisms such as ant colonies, forests, and
nested living systems, and asks how to define an organism in a scale-free way.
The key move is to stop treating an organism as a bounded object with fixed
parts and instead describe it as a reflexive hierarchy: parts act as organs of
larger organisms, while larger organisms provide context, constraint, and
meaning for their parts.

The paper introduces the mechanistic vocabulary that later gets compressed into
RC: fast and slow fields, assembly, experience, memory, compatibility,
reflexive closure, hierarchy, parent-child communication, internal time, and
the idea that identity is a dynamic invariant rather than a static boundary.

Its main contribution is not the final RC formalism. Its contribution is the
organismal problem statement: a theory of life-like systems must explain how a
system can be self-contained, internally reflective, scale-free, and still able
to change without losing itself.

### [Seeds of Life](2025-11-SeedsOfLife.md)

This paper asks what guides a reflexive organism once the basic ROM loop exists.
If the model can choose between building, exploring, compressing, and
preserving, what determines which structures are available or natural for it to
express?

The answer developed here is the language of reserves, seeds, attractor basins,
and sparks. Reserves describe how much transformation the system can undertake
without breaking coherence. Seeds are reusable structural patterns that bias
future development. Eigenmodes and attractor basins explain why some patterns
become stable enough to be recalled, reused, and extended. Sparks describe the
events where a system crosses a threshold and a new basin or identity can form.

The main contribution is the bridge from abstract reflexive dynamics to
generative structure. The paper explains why systems do not explore randomly:
they reuse seeds, deepen basins, spend reserves, and sometimes spark into new
forms.

### [Coherence in Reflexive Organism Model](2025-11-Coherence.md)

This paper identifies coherence as the central invariant hidden inside the ROM
vocabulary. Earlier ROM language treats assembly, experience, compatibility,
memory, reserves, and attractors as distinct interacting pieces. This paper
shows that they can be understood as different expressions of one deeper
quantity: coherence.

The paper develops coherence as a scalar field and geometric quantity. It
introduces the coherence continuity equation, coherence flux, and coherence
tensor, then interprets seeds, eigenmodes, plateaus, destructive agents, and
emotional/motivational dynamics as consequences of how coherence is conserved,
redistributed, stressed, or stabilized.

Its main contribution is the conceptual compression that makes RC possible.
Instead of asking how many separate ROM components need to be coordinated, it
asks whether identity, memory, experience, and geometry can all be read as modes
of a single coherence field.

### [Reflexive Coherence](2025-11-ReflexiveCoherence.md)

This is the compact coherence-only formulation. It proposes that the primitive
state is:

```text
S_coh = (C(x,t), J_C(x,t))
```

where `C(x,t)` is coherence density and `J_C` is coherence flux. From this pair,
the paper derives the objects that ROM had treated separately: domain, action
alphabet, graph, organ states, memory, sensing, policy, actuation,
reproduction/pruning, assembly, experience, proper time, and coarse-graining.

The central loop is:

```text
C -> K[C] -> g[K] -> J_C -> continuity -> C
```

The field induces geometry. Geometry constrains flux. Flux updates the field.
The updated field changes the geometry again. The system is therefore
self-referential at the level of its own metric and dynamics.

The main contribution is the formal reduction of ROM into one coherence field
and its induced geometry. This is the point where Geometric Reflexive Coherence
becomes a field theory rather than only an organismal model.

### [Reflexive Coherence: A Geometric Theory of Identity, Choice, and Abundance](2025-11-RC-IdentityChoiceAbundance.md)

This paper interprets what the coherence-only formulation means for identity
and agency. Identity becomes an invariant basin of the coherence field. Choice
becomes a collapse from multiple compatible continuations into one continuation.
Learning becomes an irreversible update to the geometry. Sparks become generic
curvature or basin bifurcations. Abundance becomes the creation of differentiated
capacity rather than mere survival or optimization.

The key claim is the observer asymmetry. From the perspective of the whole
field, the dynamics may be deterministic. From the perspective of any part of
the field, the next global state is computationally irreducible. No local part
can fully predict the whole without being the whole. This gives a geometric
interpretation of uncertainty, agency, and the lived experience of choice.

The main contribution is the translation from field dynamics into agency
language without adding an external controller. Agency is not imposed on the
system. It is the local experience of irreducible self-continuation inside a
self-generated geometry.

### [Fractal Reflexive Coherence](2025-11-FractalReflexiveCoherence.md)

This paper addresses a limitation of single-field RC: one loop can describe a
coherent identity, but living, cognitive, social, and ecological systems are
nested. Identity often persists not at one scale but through coupled
sub-identities across many scales.

Fractal Reflexive Coherence introduces a generation or scale coordinate and
promotes coherence from `C(x,t)` to a scale-resolved field `C(x,t;sigma)`. This
allows coherence to move not only through position but also through scale. The
paper develops scale flux, cascading conservation, multiscale identity basins,
and attractor tubes that persist through nested hierarchy.

The main contribution is the multiscale completion of the RC loop. It explains
how identity can propagate through nested structures without reducing the
system to a flat single-level field or to disconnected local loops.

### [The Continuation Spectrum](2026-08-TheContinuationSpectrum.md)

This paper asks what lies between a formed identity's present configuration
and the finite attractor basins into which it may later resolve. Around a
constrained formed state, it defines the continuation operator from the second
variation of the RC functional. Its spectrum distinguishes hard, soft,
marginal, and unstable directions of possible next continuation.

The paper also separates structural stiffness from temporal relaxation. On an
explicitly declared conserved-density gradient-flow branch, moving-volume
kinematics and mobility produce a distinct relaxation spectrum and a
dynamically slow sector. Its main contribution is a local geometry of capacity:
what the present formation can resist, accommodate, or approach as a loss of
restoring adequacy.

### [Read-Back](2026-08-ReadBack.md)

This paper asks how retained continuation becomes active in present flow. It
reconstructs a coherence-only read-back relation from the ROM-to-RC lineage,
keeps state-level spatial retention distinct from branch-relative dynamical
retention, and develops a mode-selective one-form response as a candidate
realization.

Its main contribution is the closed read/write relation between retained
formation and present activity. Read-back gain describes immediate kinetic
accessibility, while repeated current can alter retained amplitudes, geometry,
and later continuation. The paper keeps this explicit-current branch distinct
from the companion paper's gradient-flow relaxation branch until a unified
constitutive generator is derived.

## Conceptual Progression

The core sequence can be read as a compression path:

```text
ROM
  -> seeds, reserves, basins, sparks
  -> coherence as invariant
  -> coherence-only field formulation
  -> identity, choice, abundance
  -> fractal/multiscale coherence
  -> local continuation geometry
  -> retained continuation as read-back
```

Or as a set of questions:

- ROM asks: what is a self-contained scale-free organism?
- Seeds asks: what makes some futures available to that organism?
- Coherence asks: what invariant holds the organism together?
- Reflexive Coherence asks: can the whole model be derived from one field?
- Identity/Choice/Abundance asks: what does that field mean for agency?
- Fractal RC asks: how does identity persist across scale?
- Continuation Spectrum asks: what can a formed identity become next?
- Read-Back asks: how does retained formation participate in present flow?
