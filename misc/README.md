# Miscellaneous And Historical Notes

The `misc/` directory contains historical or transitional material that
remains useful for understanding how parts of the Reflexive Coherence program
developed but does not belong to a current cumulative paper sequence.

Documents here should not be treated as the latest statement of the RC
formalism or its interpretive vocabulary. Follow their links and terminology
with the date and historical context in mind.

## Contents

### [Reflexive Loop: A Framework For Balancing Building And Learning](2025-10-Living-ROM.md)

This early ROM-era note maps assembly, experience, daily activity, and learning
loops into a personal and practical interpretation of the Reflexive Organism
Model. It records intuitions that later developed into the Arc of Becoming,
Reflexive Coherence Development, and the participation-oriented papers.

**Status:** historical and exploratory; retained for provenance rather than as
a canonical guide to the current theory.
