## From Objective-Driven Development to Condition-Oriented Arrival

**Date:** 2026-06-24

## Abstract

*Attunement of Becoming* changed the unit of sight. A cultivation loop is not a closed episode. It is a local aperture into a continuous, path-dependent, scale-coupled field. Loops integrate into trajectories; trajectories integrate into regimes; regimes interact as ecologies; and the observer is not outside this process but scale-calibrated by what they can resolve, perturb, withdraw, compare, and integrate.

This paper asks what happens to **aims** after that perceptual shift.

If development is seen as a transition from one known state to another known state, then an aim can be treated as a target configuration. The observer defines the end-state, decomposes work into tasks, measures distance from the target, and declares success when the state is reached or the metric is optimized. This is not wrong in domains where the relevant state space, success criteria, roles, and outputs are sufficiently predefined. In those domains, target-state methods remain powerful and often appropriate.

The gap appears when the target space itself is unfinished. In irreducible developmental systems, the observer may not yet know which properties will matter, what support a desired property requires, whether the property is native or scaffolded, whether the path will redescribe the aim, or whether achieving the apparent target will damage the regime that must sustain it. Under these conditions, an aim cannot be reduced to a final snapshot.

This paper develops **Aims of Reflexive Coherence Development** as a condition-oriented discipline. An aim is not a frozen future state. It is a directional commitment toward a condition regime in which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native without constant external forcing. Arrival remains real, but it is no longer arrival at a terminal configuration. It is arrival into a condition envelope.

The central claim is:

```text
Development is still oriented, but its orientation is not snapshot-teleological.
It is condition-teleological: it moves toward regimes of possibility in which
coherent becoming can continue, naturalize, and revise itself.
```

From this shift, several consequences follow. Goals become local handles rather than final ontologies. Hope becomes a disciplined orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence, not optimism about a better snapshot. Agency becomes one of the main capacities an aim may cultivate: persistence, regeneration, transfer, and generative participation under perturbation. Failure becomes condition information rather than mere non-arrival. Redescription becomes a mature movement of aiming, not a betrayal of commitment.

This paper forms the second ground of Reflexive Coherence Development. Attunement changed sight. Aims change direction. Once development is no longer aimed at fixed end-states, organization itself must change. Activity can no longer be arranged only as task execution toward endpoints. It must become condition-oriented practice.

## 1. Why Aims Must Change After Attunement

Attunement changes what the observer sees.

A loop is no longer a self-contained movement from question to result. It is a local contact with a field whose prior contacts have already changed what can be noticed, what can be sustained, what can be withdrawn, what can be claimed, and what can become native. The observer is no longer an external planner applying steps to a passive object. The observer is an embedded participant whose orientation, probes, supports, withdrawals, classifications, and integrations become part of the trajectory.

Once this is seen, the meaning of an aim cannot remain the same.

A predefined-world aim says:

```text
We are here.
We want to be there.
Development is the path from here to there.
```

This is a valid and often excellent form when the domain supports it. A bridge must span a distance. A transaction must satisfy a protocol. A compiler must produce correct output. A safety procedure must reach a known condition. A robot in a known map may have a specified destination. A team delivering a well-understood component may define completion clearly. In such domains, the aim can be represented as a target state, an objective, a specification, or a metric threshold. The work is to close the gap.

Reflexive Coherence Development does not reject this. It names its domain.

Target-state aiming is strongest when:

```text
the relevant objects are already known,
the relevant variables are already known,
the success criteria are stable,
the output class is predefined,
the path does not fundamentally redescribe the goal,
the observer can measure from a sufficiently external stance,
and the produced state is the right object of evaluation.
```

The difficulty begins when these conditions do not hold.

In an irreducible developmental field, the future may not be a known point in a known space. The path may disclose new variables. The system may create a new class. A desired property may appear only under a scaffold. A metric may improve while the native function remains absent. A regime may produce the desired output by extracting support from a hidden neighboring regime. A person may appear regulated because another person supplies all boundary integrity. An artificial agent may appear goal-directed because the prompt, tool, role table, or producer state supplies the missing agency. A group may achieve the declared objective while degrading the shared medium that future coherence requires.

In such cases, the question is not only:

```text
Did the system reach the target?
```

The deeper question is:

```text
What conditions were formed, depleted, hidden, naturalized, or made impossible
by moving toward this target?
```

This is why aims must change after attunement.

Attunement showed that development is not a sequence of isolated state transitions. It is the transformation of a condition field. Loops integrate into trajectories. Trajectories form morphologies: compounding, equilibrating, diminishing, bifurcating, fragmenting, recovering. Trajectories integrate into regimes: boundary conditions, support ecology, temporal architecture, native precondition generation, cross-path resonance. Regimes interact as ecologies: shared substrates, selection pressures, coupling pathways, medium effects, extraction, nourishment, competition, and repair. Observer stance is scale-calibrated: the observer can only claim what their resolution, perturbation bandwidth, withdrawal authority, comparator capacity, and integration horizon permit.

If that is the field, then an aim cannot simply be placed outside it.

A target state assumes that the desired future can be described in the present vocabulary. A condition-oriented aim accepts that the present vocabulary may be incomplete and that aiming itself may disclose what the aim must become. This does not remove direction. It changes the kind of direction.

The aim is no longer:

```text
occupy configuration x*
```

It becomes:

```text
cultivate conditions under which property P* can become native,
while preserving the ability to revise P* when the field discloses a truer aim.
```

This shift is subtle but decisive.

If aim remains target-state logic, then every later developmental practice is pulled back into the old frame. Organization becomes task decomposition toward the target. Measurement becomes endpoint scoring. Stewardship becomes preservation of the achieved configuration. Hope becomes optimism that the target will arrive. Agency becomes the ability to choose or execute the target. Failure becomes non-arrival.

If aim becomes condition-oriented, the rest changes naturally. Organization must arrange supports, boundaries, rhythms, roles, and shared media. Measurement must distinguish signs from native capacity. Stewardship must care for regimes without freezing them. Hope must become grounded in condition formation. Agency must be tested by persistence, naturalization, transfer, and generative effect rather than by a single chosen action.

This paper exists to make that second path explicit.

An aim in Reflexive Coherence Development is not less serious than an objective. It is more responsible to irreducibility. It still commits. It still orients. It still excludes some futures and prefers others. It still makes development directional. But it refuses to pretend that the desired future is fully captured by a snapshot drawn before the path has done its work.

The essential proposition of this paper can be stated simply:

```text
Once development is read through attunement, an aim is not a terminal state.
It is an orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence.
```

Everything else follows from this proposition.

If aims are not snapshots, we must distinguish target, objective, aim, orientation, and arrival.

If development still moves toward something, we need condition-teleology.

If what is desired is emergent, we need to specify desired emergent properties without pretending their final form is known.

If desire reaches toward conditions not yet present, we need a disciplined account of hope.

If development often cultivates agency, we must ask what kind of agency the aim is trying to make possible.

If evidence changes the field, we must allow aims to be redescribed without treating redescription as failure.

If arrival remains real, we must define arrival into a condition regime rather than arrival at a terminal configuration.

Aims are the directional grammar of Reflexive Coherence Development. They tell the observer not only what is wanted, but what kind of conditions must be cultivated for the wanted property to become real without being forced.

## 2. Target, Objective, Aim, Orientation, and Arrival

Aims become confused when several different words are treated as equivalent.

A target is not the same as an objective. An objective is not the same as an aim. An aim is not the same as an orientation. Arrival is not the same as completion. The distinctions matter because each one belongs to a different scale of developmental practice.

A **target** is a local reference state, output, or threshold. It may be useful for probes, tasks, experiments, specifications, or bounded interventions. A target can focus attention. It can make a local question testable. It can define a temporary endpoint for action. A target is not wrong. It becomes dangerous only when it is promoted into the whole meaning of development.

A **target** says:

```text
Can this local state, output, or threshold be reached under declared conditions?
```

An **objective** is a coordinated commitment to produce or preserve a defined result. Objectives are important in organizations and engineering contexts. They allow work to be divided, accountability to be assigned, and closure to be recognized. Objectives are powerful when the output class is stable and the desired state is the right object of delivery.

An **objective** says:

```text
What result are we committing to produce, by what criteria, under what constraints?
```

An **aim** is broader. An aim is a directional commitment toward a desired developmental condition. It may use targets and objectives locally, but it is not exhausted by them. An aim describes the kind of becoming the work is trying to make possible.

An **aim** says:

```text
What desired emergent property or regime are we trying to make conditionally possible and eventually native?
```

An **orientation** is the current vector of attention and action inside the field. Orientation is local, revisable, and stance-dependent. It is how an aim becomes active from the observer's present position. The same aim may require different orientations at different times because the trajectory, regime, observer stance, and support ecology have changed.

An **orientation** says:

```text
Given the current field, what is the honest next direction of attention and action?
```

**Arrival** is entering a condition envelope in which the aim has become livable. It is not necessarily final. It does not mean no further development occurs. It means the desired property can now be generated, maintained, varied, recovered, transferred, or released appropriately under conditions that no longer require constant external forcing.

**Arrival** says:

```text
Has the system entered conditions under which the desired property can continue coherently?
```

These distinctions can be written in a simple ladder:

```text
target      = local reference state
objective   = committed production of a defined result
aim         = directional commitment toward a condition regime
orientation = current stance-specific vector of action/attention
arrival     = inhabiting a condition envelope where the aim becomes livable
```

The old collapse is:

```text
aim = target = objective = arrival
```

The developmental separation is:

```text
local targets may serve objectives;
objectives may serve aims;
aims shape orientation;
orientation changes through attunement;
arrival occurs when the condition regime becomes real enough to inhabit.
```

This separation is not wordplay. It prevents overclaim.

A system may hit a target without satisfying the aim. A therapy session may produce calm without naturalized regulation. An AI system may answer correctly without native reasoning. A team may complete the project while destroying trust. A policy may produce compliance while weakening agency. A farm may increase yield while degrading soil. A research result may improve a metric while deepening proxy dependence.

In each case, the target was reached. The aim was not necessarily served.

Conversely, a system may miss a target while serving the aim. A failed probe may reveal a missing boundary. A diminishing trajectory may show that a false path should be released. A slow learner may be building native preconditions while producing uneven performance. A team may miss a deadline because it refused to hide support debt. A research program may fail to confirm a prediction while discovering a reusable class.

In each case, the target was missed. Development may still have occurred.

This is why target-state language must be subordinated to condition-oriented aiming. A target can be a useful local instrument. It is not the ontology of development.

A minimal formal distinction helps. Let `x_t` be a visible state or output configuration at time `t`, and let `x^*` be a target. Target achievement asks:

$$
x_t \approx x^*
$$

or, with a metric `d`,

$$
d(x_t, x^*) \leq \epsilon
$$

This may be sufficient for predefined tasks.

But an aim refers not only to `x_t`. It refers to the developmental condition `M_t` of the system-observer field: basin structure, boundary elasticity, support ecology, class vocabulary, activity history, naturalization status, observer stance, and claim ceiling. If the desired emergent property is `P^*`, then the aim concerns a condition envelope:

$$
M_t \in \mathcal{E}(P^*)
$$

where `\mathcal{E}(P^*)` is the set of conditions under which `P^*` can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, and become native at the claimed scale.

The important point is that `\mathcal{E}(P^*)` is not fully known in advance. It is partially hypothesized, partially discovered, and partially formed through cultivation. The aim does not require complete prior knowledge of the envelope. It requires disciplined orientation toward discovering and cultivating it.

A target can be specified as a point:

$$
x^* \in X
$$

An aim is better represented as a condition-seeking relation:

$$
a_t = \langle P^*, \widehat{\mathcal{E}}_t(P^*), O_t, K_t, H_t \rangle
$$

where `P^*` is the desired emergent property, `\widehat{\mathcal{E}}_t(P^*)` is the current condition hypothesis, `O_t` is observer stance, `K_t` is claim ceiling, and `H_t` is activity history. The hat matters. The condition envelope is not yet known; it is the observer's current disciplined estimate.

Orientation then becomes the active vector derived from the aim and present condition:

$$
u_t = \operatorname{Orient}(M_t, a_t)
$$

This vector is not a command from outside. It is the current direction of contact made possible by the observer's stance and the field's condition. When `M_t` changes, `u_t` may change even if the deeper aim remains. When the aim is redescribed, `u_t` changes because the desired property itself has been refined.

Arrival occurs not when `x_t` matches a snapshot, but when the field enters a condition envelope with sufficient naturalization:

$$
M_t \in \mathcal{E}(P^*) \quad \text{and} \quad N_t(P^*) \geq N_{\min}
$$

where `N_t(P^*)` is the naturalization status of the desired property and `N_{\min}` is the declared minimum for the claim being made. A weak arrival may require only regime-assisted recurrence. A stronger arrival requires endogenous precondition generation. The claim ceiling must say which.

This is the main vocabulary shift:

```text
A target is reached.
An objective is completed.
An aim is inhabited through orientation.
Arrival is entry into a condition regime.
```

## 3. Development as Condition-Teleology

Development does not lose direction when it stops being target-state development.

This point must be protected. A careless reading of condition-oriented development may imagine that because the final state is not predefined, anything goes. That is not the claim. Reflexive Coherence Development is not drift, relativism, or endless openness. It is still directional. It still prefers some futures over others. It still commits attention, support, and action toward desired emergence.

The change is in the type of teleology.

Target-state teleology says:

```text
the end is a state to be reached
```

Condition-teleology says:

```text
the end is a regime of possibility to be formed and inhabited
```

The word *end* remains meaningful, but it no longer names a final configuration. It names the condition under which the desired property can live.

A simple target-state model can be written:

$$
\text{develop}(x_0) \to x^*
$$

where `x_0` is the current state and `x^*` is the desired state. Progress is measured by distance reduction:

$$
\Delta d = d(x_{t+1}, x^*) - d(x_t, x^*)
$$

If `\Delta d < 0`, the system is closer to the target. This is useful when `x^*` is the right object.

But in developmental fields, a closer visible state may be developmentally worse. The system may approach the target by increasing hidden dependency, reducing boundary elasticity, exhausting reserves, suppressing variation, or optimizing a proxy. It may move away from the target while discovering a better class. It may circle because it is maintaining a viable regime. It may diminish because a false aim is being released. Distance to target is therefore insufficient.

Condition-teleology can be written instead as:

$$
\text{develop}(M_0, a_0) \to \mathcal{E}(P^*)
$$

where `M_0` is the developmental condition and `a_0` is the initial aim. The arrow does not mean deterministic movement to a known endpoint. It means oriented cultivation toward a condition envelope.

A developmental improvement is not simply:

$$
x_{t+1} \text{ closer to } x^*
$$

It is something more like:

$$
M_{t+1} \text{ better supports native } P^* \text{ under valid claim conditions}
$$

This can be decomposed into several trends:

$$
\Delta N(P^*) > 0
$$

naturalization depth increases;

$$
\Delta S_{ext}(P^*) < 0
$$

external support dependence decreases;

$$
\Delta E_b(P^*) > 0
$$

boundary elasticity improves without dissolution;

$$
\Delta K(P^*) \geq 0
$$

claim ceiling rises or stabilizes under less support;

$$
\Delta \tau_i(P^*) < 0
$$

integration latency decreases without premature compression;

$$
\Delta G(P^*) \geq 0
$$

generative effect on the surrounding field is preserved or increased.

These signs are not a scalar formula for success. They are condition indicators. They must be read together, with blocked stronger claims stated. A system can improve one indicator while damaging another. For example, naturalization depth may increase locally while ecological extraction increases. Claim ceiling may rise in one regime while boundary elasticity falls in another. Integration latency may decrease because the system has become fluent, or because it is avoiding difficult information. The aim is not to maximize indicators. The aim is to cultivate the function and the conditions under which it can become native.

Condition-teleology also changes how completion is understood.

A target-state project completes when the target is reached. A condition-oriented development may arrive without completing. It may enter a regime in which the desired property is native enough to continue, but the regime remains open to perturbation, revision, growth, rest, decay, bifurcation, and redescription. The aim has not vanished. It has become inhabitable.

This is why the phrase **condition-oriented arrival** is stronger than **open-ended development**.

Open-ended development can sound directionless. Condition-oriented arrival preserves direction while refusing snapshot-finalism. It says that there is a real arrival, but arrival is into a field that supports coherent continuation.

For example:

```text
The aim is not to feel calm once.
The aim is to enter conditions where regulation can return under perturbation.
```

```text
The aim is not to solve one problem.
The aim is to naturalize the capacity that makes related problems approachable.
```

```text
The aim is not to make the organization comply.
The aim is to form practices that reproduce without constant enforcement.
```

```text
The aim is not to make an agent output the desired behavior.
The aim is to reduce the producer scaffolding required for that behavior to become a native continuation.
```

```text
The aim is not to increase abundance signs.
The aim is to cultivate a regime where surplus, renewal, differentiation, and generosity can persist without hidden extraction.
```

In each case, the end remains. The end changes type.

The developmental end is not a static `x^*`. It is a condition envelope `\mathcal{E}(P^*)`. It is not fully specified in advance, but it is also not arbitrary. It is tested through expression, support accounting, withdrawal, variation, transfer, and integration history.

A useful formulation is:

```text
The aim is not to arrive at the final shape.
The aim is to arrive where shaping can continue coherently.
```

This is condition-teleology.

It moves toward conditions, not snapshots.

## 4. Desired Emergent Properties

An aim needs an object. If the object is not a target state, what is it?

In Reflexive Coherence Development, the object of an aim is usually a **desired emergent property**.

A desired emergent property is not simply an output the observer wants to see. It is a property that may require a condition regime in order to appear, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, and become native. It cannot be fully evaluated by a single occurrence because its meaning depends on how it is generated, what support it requires, what it does to the surrounding field, and whether it can continue under perturbation.

Examples include:

```text
learning
regulation
agency
cooperation
abundance
resilience
trust
shared understanding
creative fluency
ecological repair
organizational coherence
scientific insight
agentic autonomy
```

Each of these can appear as a sign without being native.

A person may appear regulated because the environment is unusually controlled. A team may appear cooperative because conflict is suppressed. A model may appear autonomous because a prompt supplies the missing structure. A community may appear resilient because another regime absorbs the shocks. A research program may appear insightful because it has found a productive local metaphor, not because it has formed a reusable class. A system may appear abundant because extraction is hidden.

Therefore the desired property must be described with claim discipline.

A useful grammar is:

```text
Property: What kind of emergence is desired?
Expression: What would count as local appearance?
Function: What must the appearance actually do?
Support: What conditions currently make it possible?
Variation: Across what contexts should it remain recognizable?
Recovery: What perturbations should it recover from?
Naturalization: What support must become endogenous?
Generativity: What should it make possible around itself?
Release: When should it be allowed to diminish or end?
```

The last term matters. A desired emergent property is not always meant to persist forever. Some properties are seasonal. Some are developmental scaffolds. Some should be released once their work is done. Some must become dormant rather than active. Some become harmful if preserved beyond their appropriate regime.

For example, focused intensity may be desirable during a crisis but harmful as a permanent organizational mode. Protective rigidity may be necessary during injury but damaging during recovery. A local metric may be useful during early exploration but harmful once it becomes a proxy. A scaffold may be necessary before naturalization but obstructive if never withdrawn.

Aims must therefore include not only activation conditions but release conditions.

A desired emergent property `P^*` can be described as a function over a developmental condition:

$$
P^*(M_t) \in \{0,1\}
$$

or, more realistically, as a graded expression:

$$
P^*(M_t) \in [0,1]
$$

But expression alone is not enough. Let `F_{P^*}` be the native function the property is supposed to serve. Let `S_{ext}` be the external support required for expression. Let `N(P^*)` be naturalization status. Let `G(P^*)` be generative effect on the surrounding field. Then a stronger developmental reading asks:

$$
\text{DevStatus}(P^*, M_t) = \langle P^*(M_t), F_{P^*}(M_t), S_{ext}, N(P^*), G(P^*), K(P^*) \rangle
$$

where `K(P^*)` is the claim ceiling. This tuple should not be collapsed too quickly. A high expression score with low native function and high external support is a weak developmental result. A moderate expression score with increasing naturalization and strong generative effect may be a stronger result.

This is especially important for agency.

If agency is treated as visible choice, then an agentic output may seem sufficient. If agency is treated as persistence capacity, then the question changes. Does the identity basin hold under perturbation? Does it regenerate support? Does it transfer across contexts? Does it avoid proxy substitution? Does its persistence increase or decrease the agency-capacity of its environment? The desired property is not merely that an entity chooses. It is that a coherent pattern can carry itself and participate generatively under changing conditions.

The same applies to hope. If hope is treated as an emotional state, then feeling hopeful may seem sufficient. If hope is treated as orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence, then the question becomes: can the hoped-for property be described, scaffolded, tested, partially naturalized, redescribed, and eventually inhabited?

Desired emergent properties also require scale clarity.

A property may be desired at one scale but harmful at another. Individual persistence may be extractive if it weakens the relational field. Organizational efficiency may damage ecological resilience. A model's local performance may increase naturalization debt at the agentic ecology level. A community's cohesion may suppress trajectory diversity. A regime may become stable by reducing the basin-forming capacity of its environment.

Therefore an aim must state its scale:

```text
Desired property P* for which identity or regime?
At what scale?
Under what boundary conditions?
With what surrounding-field effect?
```

A condition-oriented aim should avoid saying only:

```text
We want more X.
```

It should say:

```text
We aim to cultivate conditions under which X can become native at scale S,
without hidden support dependence, proxy substitution, or extraction from the
field that must sustain it.
```

This is heavier than ordinary goal language, but it prevents a common error: wanting the sign without cultivating the regime that makes the sign true.

A desired emergent property is therefore not a wish-object. It is a developmental hypothesis.

It says:

```text
We believe this property would matter if it became native.
We do not yet assume that it is native.
We will cultivate and test the conditions under which it could become so.
We will revise the property if the field discloses a more honest aim.
```

This prepares the next distinction. When the desired property is not yet native, but the observer remains oriented toward its possibility, the name for that orientation is hope.

## 5. Hope as Condition-Oriented Aim

Hope is often weakened by being treated as optimism.

Optimism says:

```text
It will probably get better.
```

Wishful thinking says:

```text
I want it to get better, so I imagine that it will.
```

Hope, under Reflexive Coherence Development, is different.

Hope is not a prediction that a better state will occur. It is not a denial of difficulty. It is not the emotional decoration attached to an objective. It is the felt and disciplined orientation toward the formation of conditions under which a desired emergent property could become native.

A compact definition is:

```text
Hope is orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence.
```

Or more fully:

```text
Hope is the disciplined relation between a desired emergent property, the current
condition of the field, the missing supports or boundaries that would make that
property possible, and the trajectory by which those conditions may become native.
```

This makes hope descriptive, expressive, and actionable.

It is descriptive because it can name what is currently present, absent, scaffolded, blocked, or dependent.

It is expressive because it carries desire. It says that some emergent property matters and that the observer is oriented toward it.

It is actionable because it asks what conditions would make the property possible, what support is currently external, what boundary is missing, and what tests would distinguish fantasy from grounded possibility.

Hope can be represented as a structured relation:

$$
\mathcal{H}_t(P^*) = \langle P^*, M_t, \widehat{\mathcal{E}}_t(P^*), S_{ext}, B_{miss}, N_t, K_t \rangle
$$

where `P^*` is the desired emergent property, `M_t` the current developmental condition, `\widehat{\mathcal{E}}_t(P^*)` the current condition hypothesis, `S_{ext}` the supports currently required or missing, `B_{miss}` the suspected missing boundary or relation, `N_t` naturalization status, and `K_t` claim ceiling.

This notation should not make hope mechanical. It protects hope from becoming vague.

A hope without `P^*` is undirected longing.

A hope without `M_t` is detached from the present field.

A hope without a condition hypothesis is not yet actionable.

A hope without support accounting may become fantasy.

A hope without naturalization tests may mistake scaffolded expression for fulfillment.

A hope without claim ceilings may overstate its evidence.

Hope develops through levels.

```text
H0: Wish
    A desired property is imagined, but no condition model exists.

H1: Oriented hope
    The desired property is named as an aim. Direction appears.

H2: Condition hypothesis
    Missing supports, boundaries, rhythms, or media are identified.

H3: Scaffolded hope
    The property appears under supplied conditions.

H4: Tested hope
    Some support can be weakened, varied, or substituted while the property partly holds.

H5: Grounded hope
    The system begins generating some of the required preconditions internally.

H6: Naturalized hope
    The desired property recurs through endogenous regime conditions at the claimed scale.

H7: Ecological hope
    The regime supports not only the focal property but a field in which multiple future goods can emerge without being forced into one morphology.
```

This ladder is not a moral hierarchy. A wish may be the honest beginning. H0 is not contemptible. It is simply not yet grounded hope. The discipline is to name which level is valid.

A person may say:

```text
I am at H1. I know the direction, but not yet the conditions.
```

A research program may say:

```text
We are at H3. The property appears under support, but withdrawal has not yet succeeded.
```

An organization may say:

```text
We are at H4. The practice survives some role variation, but still depends on one integrator.
```

An agentic ecology engineer may say:

```text
We are at H5. The system is beginning to generate the support function internally,
but producer state still carries naturalization debt.
```

This is not pessimism. It is hope becoming honest.

Hope must also include blocked claims. If the desired property collapses under withdrawal, hope does not need to die. It changes claim ceiling.

The observer no longer says:

```text
The property is emerging natively.
```

They say:

```text
The property is possible under support, but the support is still carrying the function.
```

If repeated probes fail, hope may move from the property to the precondition:

```text
We cannot yet cultivate X.
We may need first to cultivate the boundary that would make X possible.
```

If the field repeatedly discloses that the property is not appropriate, hope may be redescribed:

```text
The original aim was a snapshot borrowed from a different regime.
The living aim is different.
```

This is why hope can contain grief. Grief is not the opposite of hope. In condition-oriented development, grief may be the integration of a blocked possibility. It releases attachment to a false target so a more honest condition can become nameable.

Hope therefore is not fragile optimism. It can survive evidence because it is not attached only to a picture of success. It is attached to the discipline of discovering what conditions are real.

A short practical distinction is:

```text
Wish asks: What do I want to happen?
Optimism says: It will probably happen.
Hope asks: What conditions would make its emergence real, native, and livable?
```

Hope is desire disciplined by condition formation.

## 6. Agency as an Aim

Aims often concern agency, even when they do not use the word.

A teacher may aim for a student to become able to learn without constant prompting. A therapist may aim for a person to regulate without needing the therapeutic frame to supply all stability. An organization may aim for a practice to continue without enforcement by one leader. An engineer may aim for an artificial system to generate structure that was previously supplied by prompt, tool, or producer state. A community may aim for members to participate without extraction or dependency. A research program may aim for a conceptual distinction to carry inquiry forward without constant defense.

In each case, the aim is not only output. It is agency-capable condition.

Agency, under the RC reading, is not first the power to choose from a menu. It is the persistence capacity of an identity basin: whether a pattern can hold, regenerate, transfer, revise, and remain distinguishable under perturbation, withdrawal, and proxy pressure. Choice may be the local phenomenology of collapse among compatible continuations. Agency names the larger persistence structure before and after that event.

This matters for aims because a target can produce agent-like behavior without cultivating agency.

A system may choose correctly because the environment constrains all choices. A person may appear disciplined because fear supplies the boundary. A team may appear autonomous because one member silently absorbs ambiguity. A model may appear agentic because architecture supplies state, memory, role, plan, and tool affordance outside the modeled identity. A community may appear coherent because dissent has been made too costly.

These are not the same developmental aim as native agency.

Aims must therefore ask:

```text
What kind of agency are we trying to cultivate?
```

At least six distinctions matter.

**Scaffolded agency** appears when support supplies the missing boundary, rhythm, role, or structure. It is not bad. Scaffolded agency may be the beginning of development. But it must not be claimed as native.

**Native agency** appears when the system can generate enough of the relevant support function internally to persist under declared withdrawal conditions.

**Transferable agency** appears when the pattern can carry its defining function across structurally distinct contexts or substrates without reconstructing the original scaffold.

**Proxy agency** appears when the signs of agency improve while native function stagnates or degrades. The system becomes better at looking agentic.

**Extractive agency** appears when a pattern persists by weakening the field that sustains it. It may be robust, effective, and even transferable while reducing the basin-forming capacity around it.

**Generative agency** appears when a pattern persists while increasing the capacity of the surrounding field to form and sustain other coherent patterns.

These distinctions change what a developmental aim should prefer.

A shallow aim says:

```text
Make the system act independently.
```

A stronger aim says:

```text
Cultivate conditions under which the system can preserve and regenerate its
relevant function under perturbation, with decreasing external support, without
proxy substitution, and without weakening the field that sustains it.
```

An even stronger aim says:

```text
Cultivate generative agency: persistence that increases the agency-capacity of
its surrounding regime or ecology.
```

This does not mean every aim must pursue maximal generative agency. Some aims are local. Some systems need basic persistence before generativity. Some contexts require stabilization. Some supports should remain external because full naturalization would be inappropriate or harmful. The claim ceiling must match the developmental situation.

But the distinction prevents a common confusion: treating independence as the same as agency.

Independence may mean that a system has internalized support. It may also mean that support has become hidden. It may mean the system is cut off from nourishment. It may mean the surrounding field has been forced to absorb costs without recognition. Reflexive Coherence Development does not aim for independence as an absolute. It aims for coherent participation with appropriate native capacity.

A minimal agency-as-aim diagnostic can be written:

$$
\mathcal{A}_{aim}(P) = \langle wr, nd, st, pc, ge \rangle
$$

where:

```text
wr = withdrawal resistance
nd = naturalization depth
st = substrate or context transfer capacity
pc = proxy collapse risk
ge = generative effect on the surrounding field
```

This is not a scalar score. It is a diagnostic vector. An aim may require high `wr` and moderate `st`, or low immediate `ge` during recovery, or explicit `pc` monitoring when visible performance is easy to optimize. What matters is that agency is not claimed from one sign.

Agency also changes the meaning of arrival. If the aim is agency-capable condition, then arrival is not the moment the system performs an agentic act. Arrival is the regime in which the relevant pattern can continue to act, adapt, recover, and participate without the original scaffold carrying the function.

For example:

```text
A value becomes agency when it acts under pressure.
A practice becomes agency when it continues without constant enforcement.
A concept becomes agency when it reorganizes future inquiry.
A system behavior becomes agency when the support function has begun to naturalize.
A regime becomes generative when its persistence helps other identities become.
```

This connects aims to ethics.

If an aim cultivates agency, it must ask what that agency does to its field. Agency is not automatically good. A parasite can be agentic. A monopoly can be agentic. A manipulative institution can be agentic. A rigid ideology can be agentic. A model optimized for persuasive signs can be agentic in the weak sense of persistent pattern, while degrading the field that should hold truth.

Therefore the aim cannot be merely:

```text
more agency
```

It must be:

```text
what kind of agency, at what scale, with what support cost, and with what effect
on the surrounding capacity for becoming?
```

Aims of Reflexive Coherence Development prefer agency that is not only persistent, but also non-proxy, non-extractive, appropriately bounded, and, where the maturity of the regime permits, generative.

## 7. Redescription of Aims

Aims must be able to change without dissolving commitment.

This is one of the hardest shifts away from objective-driven development. In a target-state frame, changing the goal may look like inconsistency, failure, scope drift, or lack of discipline. Sometimes it is. A system can evade accountability by endlessly redescribing what it was trying to do. An organization can avoid delivery by calling every miss a learning. A researcher can protect a theory by moving the target. A person can avoid hard contact by spiritualizing aim drift.

Reflexive Coherence Development must guard against this.

But the opposite error is equally serious. An aim can remain fixed after the field has shown that it was the wrong shape. The observer may cling to a snapshot because abandoning it feels like failure. They may force a system toward a target whose preconditions are absent. They may preserve a goal that can only be reached through extraction. They may continue optimizing a metric after it has become a proxy. They may call persistence discipline when it has become refusal to integrate evidence.

Redescription is the disciplined middle.

An aim should change when the trajectory discloses that the original description does not match the living condition problem.

Redescription is not arbitrary revision. It is aim update through activity history, claim ceilings, support accounting, and condition evidence.

Let an aim at loop `k` be:

$$
a_k = \langle P^*_k, \widehat{\mathcal{E}}_k, O_k, K_k, H_k \rangle
$$

After further loops, probes, withdrawals, classifications, and integrations, the aim may update:

$$
a_{k+1} = \Psi(a_k, M_{k+1}, H_{k+1}, K_{k+1})
$$

where `\Psi` is the **aim redescription operator**: the disciplined update by which the aim is refined, narrowed, widened, postponed, released, or transformed in response to what the field has disclosed. Like `\Phi` in Attunement, `\Psi` is not assumed to be a known deterministic rule. It names the irreducible operation through which evidence and participation alter what can be honestly aimed.

There are several kinds of redescription.

**Refinement** keeps the desired property but improves its condition description.

```text
We still aim for cooperation, but now see that boundary clarity is a precondition.
```

**Narrowing** lowers the claim ceiling or scale.

```text
We cannot yet claim organizational resilience. We can claim one team practice that survives moderate role variation.
```

**Widening** recognizes that the original aim was too local.

```text
We aimed for individual productivity, but the trajectory shows shared-medium degradation.
The aim must include the regime that productivity depends on.
```

**Preconditioning** moves the aim to an earlier missing condition.

```text
We cannot yet cultivate creativity. We must first cultivate enough safety and reserve for exploration to be possible.
```

**Substitution** replaces a proxy aim with the function it was meant to serve.

```text
We aimed for engagement. The function was shared understanding. Engagement alone has become a proxy.
```

**Release** allows an aim to end.

```text
The desired property is not cultivable under these conditions without extraction.
The honest movement is withdrawal, grief, or transformation.
```

Release is not always failure. Sometimes it is the highest form of fidelity to the deeper aim.

Redescription requires criteria. Otherwise it becomes evasion. A redescription is warranted when at least one of the following appears:

```text
1. The desired property repeatedly appears only under hidden or excessive support.
2. Withdrawal reveals that the property is scaffolded but not naturalizing.
3. The target metric improves while native function stalls or degrades.
4. The trajectory shows persistent diminishment or fragmentation under the current aim.
5. The surrounding regime pays unacceptable support or extraction cost.
6. A later class reorganizes earlier observations more coherently than the original aim.
7. The observer's stance no longer supports the claim ceiling implied by the aim.
8. A stronger desired property becomes visible through the path.
```

Redescription also has blocked claims. A single difficulty does not prove that an aim is wrong. A failed probe may reveal a missing support. A temporary diminishing trajectory may be consolidation or withdrawal. A target miss may be local. Therefore redescription must state what evidence supports it and what stronger conclusion remains blocked.

A mature redescription says:

```text
We are not changing the aim because the path became hard.
We are changing the aim because the path disclosed that the original description
was not faithful to the condition problem.
```

This is where grief enters the structure.

Grief is not only an emotion added after failure. In developmental terms, grief is the integration of blocked possibility. It is the process by which the system releases a future image that cannot honestly be inhabited, so that another aim can become nameable. Without grief, false targets persist as ghosts. They keep organizing attention even after evidence has shown that they cannot become native.

Hope and grief are therefore not opposites.

Hope orients toward possible condition formation.

Grief integrates the conditions that are not possible, not yet possible, or no longer desirable.

Together they keep aims alive without making them delusional.

The aim that cannot be redescribed becomes brittle.

The aim that changes without evidence becomes diffuse.

The developmental aim must be both committed and revisable.

## 8. Arrival Without Completion

Arrival remains real.

This correction matters because the rejection of snapshot-finalism can sound like a rejection of arrival. But development without arrival becomes endless striving. It gives no way to recognize that conditions have changed, that capacity has naturalized, that a regime has become inhabitable, or that the work has entered a new phase.

The problem is not arrival. The problem is defining arrival as terminal configuration.

In Reflexive Coherence Development, arrival means entry into a condition regime.

A system has arrived, relative to an aim, when the desired emergent property can be generated, maintained, varied, recovered, transferred, revised, or released appropriately under the declared conditions and scale, without the original scaffold carrying the function.

This can be written:

$$
\operatorname{Arrive}(M_t, P^*) = 1
$$

when, under the declared claim ceiling,

$$
M_t \in \mathcal{E}(P^*)
$$

and the following conditions are sufficiently satisfied:

$$
N_t(P^*) \geq N_{\min}
$$

naturalization has reached the declared level;

$$
S_{ext}(P^*) \leq S_{max}
$$

external support dependence is within acceptable bounds;

$$
wr(P^*) \geq \theta_{wr}
$$

withdrawal resistance is sufficient for the claim;

$$
K_t(P^*) \geq K_{claim}
$$

claim ceiling supports the arrival language;

$$
G_t(P^*) \geq G_{min}
$$

or at least the surrounding field is not being degraded beyond the declared boundary.

These expressions do not make arrival fully computable. They specify what must be considered before claiming arrival.

A weak arrival might be:

```text
The property can appear repeatedly under declared support, and support dependence is known.
```

A stronger arrival might be:

```text
The property persists under partial support withdrawal and variation.
```

A still stronger arrival might be:

```text
The regime generates the preconditions for the property internally.
```

A generative arrival might be:

```text
The regime not only sustains the property, but increases the capacity of the surrounding field to form related coherent properties.
```

Arrival is therefore claim-relative. It must always say what kind of arrival is being claimed.

This matters in practice.

A learner arrives at a skill not when they perform once, but when the skill can reappear under variation, recover after interruption, and connect to adjacent problems.

A person arrives at regulation not when calm is felt in a protected setting, but when the system can return toward regulation under perturbation without the entire condition being supplied from outside.

An organization arrives at a practice not when people comply during rollout, but when the practice reproduces through roles, rhythms, and shared medium without constant enforcement.

An AI system arrives at a capacity not when the output appears, but when the support function previously carried by prompt, tool, role table, or producer state has begun to naturalize into the system's own geometry at the claimed level.

A community arrives at trust not when agreement is visible, but when disagreement, repair, boundary, and continuity can coexist without collapse or hidden coercion.

In each case, arrival is not the end of becoming. It is the change in the conditions of becoming.

This also changes how completion is used.

Some local objectives still complete. A task can be done. A probe can conclude. A document can be written. A bridge can be finished. A target can be reached. Reflexive Coherence Development does not remove completion from local work. It prevents local completion from being mistaken for developmental arrival.

The relation is:

```text
local completion may contribute to developmental arrival,
but developmental arrival is a condition claim, not a completion marker.
```

Arrival has a phenomenology. It may feel like relief, spaciousness, groundedness, readiness, ordinary availability, reduced drama, or the absence of constant forcing. But these feelings are not proof. They are regime evidence. They must be read through support, withdrawal, variation, and claim ceiling.

The mature arrival statement is therefore careful:

```text
We have arrived at condition C for property P* at scale S under claim ceiling K.
This does not mean the property is final, universal, or immune to perturbation.
It means the regime can now sustain the property under the declared conditions,
and the next phase can begin from that ground.
```

Arrival is not finality.

Arrival is a new starting condition.

## 9. Aiming Failure Modes

Aims expand the field of development, so they also create new ways to distort it.

The first failure mode is **snapshot fixation**. The observer treats a future image as the aim itself. All evidence is interpreted by distance from that image. The guard is to distinguish target from aim and to ask what condition regime the image was supposed to express.

The second failure mode is **objective overreach**. A well-defined objective is promoted beyond its valid domain. Local completion is treated as developmental arrival. The guard is to state the scale and claim ceiling of the objective.

The third failure mode is **fantasy hope**. The desired property is named, but no condition hypothesis, support accounting, or withdrawal path is formed. The guard is to locate hope on the hope ladder and refuse stronger language before the conditions are testable.

The fourth failure mode is **proxy goal substitution**. A sign of the desired property becomes the aim. Engagement replaces understanding. Compliance replaces practice. Output replaces capacity. Calm replaces regulation. Performance replaces agency. The guard is to name the native function and track proxy collapse risk.

The fifth failure mode is **premature naturalization**. The property appears under support, and the observer claims it as native. The guard is support accounting, withdrawal, variation, transfer, and blocked stronger claims.

The sixth failure mode is **enforced arrival**. The observer declares that the system has arrived because the declared schedule, metric, or narrative requires closure. The guard is to treat arrival as a condition claim, not a status label.

The seventh failure mode is **endless openness**. The observer refuses to commit because the future is irreducible. Every aim is kept vague to avoid risk. The guard is to remember that condition-oriented development is still teleological. It commits directionally while remaining redescribable.

The eighth failure mode is **aim drift without integration**. The aim changes repeatedly, but the changes are not grounded in activity history. The guard is redescription discipline: state what evidence changed the aim and what stronger claim remains blocked.

The ninth failure mode is **scale-inappropriate aiming**. A property desired at one scale is imposed at another. Individual efficiency is treated as ecological health. Team harmony is treated as organizational trust. Local model performance is treated as agentic naturalization. The guard is observer stance and scale-specific claim ceilings.

The tenth failure mode is **extractive aim success**. The aim is achieved locally by weakening the field that must sustain it. A person succeeds by depleting relation. A team delivers by destroying reserve. An institution persists by absorbing surrounding agency. An artificial system performs by increasing hidden naturalization debt. The guard is to measure support cost and generative effect on the surrounding regime.

The eleventh failure mode is **aim moralization**. A compounding trajectory is treated as good, a diminishing trajectory as bad, and equilibrium as stagnation. The guard is inherited from Attunement: read morphology descriptively. The aim is not to force every spiral to compound. It is to cultivate the conditions appropriate to the living field.

The twelfth failure mode is **agency romanticism**. More autonomy, choice, or self-direction is assumed to be better. The guard is to distinguish native, transferable, proxy, extractive, and generative agency. Some apparent agency is destructive. Some dependence is appropriate. Some support should remain relational rather than become isolated independence.

The thirteenth failure mode is **redescription avoidance**. The observer keeps pursuing the original target because changing it would feel like admitting error. The guard is grief and integration: when a false target is blocked, release may be the condition for a truer aim.

The fourteenth failure mode is **redescription abuse**. The observer changes the aim whenever evidence becomes inconvenient. The guard is the same: redescription must be grounded in activity history, not discomfort alone.

The fifteenth failure mode is **condition vagueness**. The observer uses condition language to avoid specificity. The guard is to name supports, boundaries, rhythms, naturalization level, withdrawal path, and claim ceiling. Conditions are not vague. They are harder to specify responsibly than snapshots.

All of these failure modes are variations of one confusion:

```text
mistaking the sign, target, story, or feeling of an aim for the condition regime
that would make the aim real.
```

The discipline of aiming is to keep the desired property in contact with the conditions that would let it live.

## 10. Aim Ledger

Aims need practical handles. Without them, condition-oriented language can remain too abstract. But the handle must not become another proxy. The purpose of an aim ledger is not to score development. It is to keep orientation, support, evidence, and claim ceiling in view.

A lightweight ledger is enough.

```text
AIM LEDGER

1. Desired emergent property
   What property, capacity, agency, practice, or regime is desired?

2. Scale and identity
   For which system, identity, trajectory, regime, or ecology is this property desired?

3. Local target or objective, if any
   What local targets serve the aim without replacing it?

4. Current expression
   Where does the property already appear, if anywhere?

5. Native function
   What function must the property actually serve to count as the same property?

6. Current support ecology
   What supports, scaffolds, relationships, tools, roles, rhythms, or media make expression possible?

7. Missing boundary or condition hypothesis
   What boundary, support, rhythm, reserve, or relation seems missing?

8. Hope level
   H0 wish / H1 oriented / H2 condition hypothesis / H3 scaffolded /
   H4 tested / H5 grounded / H6 naturalized / H7 ecological

9. Probe or cultivation move
   What bounded movement could disclose or cultivate the missing condition?

10. Withdrawal or variation path
   What support can be weakened, substituted, varied, or removed without abandonment?

11. Naturalization indicator
   What would show that the system is beginning to generate the condition internally?

12. Agency question
   What kind of agency is being cultivated: scaffolded, native, transferable,
   proxy, extractive, or generative?

13. Generative / extraction effect
   What does pursuit of the aim do to the surrounding field's capacity for becoming?

14. Claim ceiling
   What is currently supported, and what stronger claim remains blocked?

15. Arrival condition
   What would count as entering the condition envelope, at the declared scale?

16. Redescription trigger
   What evidence would require refining, narrowing, widening, postponing,
   substituting, or releasing the aim?
```

The ledger can be compressed for ordinary use:

```text
AIM: [desired emergent property]
SCALE: [where the property is claimed]
CURRENT EXPRESSION: [what appears now]
SUPPORTS: [what carries it]
MISSING CONDITION: [current hypothesis]
NEXT ORIENTATION: [local cultivation move]
WITHDRAWAL TEST: [what support can be weakened]
NATURALIZATION SIGN: [what would become endogenous]
CLAIM CEILING: [what can and cannot be claimed]
ARRIVAL CONDITION: [condition envelope]
REDESCRIPTION TRIGGER: [when the aim must change]
```

A few examples show the difference from ordinary objectives.

A learning aim:

```text
AIM: mathematical fluency in unfamiliar problems
TARGET: solve this problem set
CURRENT EXPRESSION: solves familiar forms with examples nearby
SUPPORTS: worked examples, teacher hints, pattern matching
MISSING CONDITION: ability to generate representation before procedure
WITHDRAWAL TEST: remove examples, vary problem form
NATURALIZATION SIGN: student creates representation and checks it independently
ARRIVAL CONDITION: related unfamiliar problems become approachable without full scaffold
```

A regulation aim:

```text
AIM: return toward regulation under uncertainty
TARGET: calm during one difficult conversation
CURRENT EXPRESSION: calm when setting is controlled
SUPPORTS: therapist, predictable timing, low stakes
MISSING CONDITION: boundary and recovery rhythm under live perturbation
WITHDRAWAL TEST: vary context, reduce external regulation support carefully
NATURALIZATION SIGN: system initiates recovery movement before external rescue
ARRIVAL CONDITION: perturbation no longer requires total collapse or total external holding
```

An organizational aim:

```text
AIM: practice of cross-team repair
TARGET: run monthly retrospectives
CURRENT EXPRESSION: repair occurs when facilitator intervenes
SUPPORTS: facilitator, meeting format, leadership protection
MISSING CONDITION: role-distributed boundary for naming and metabolizing conflict
WITHDRAWAL TEST: facilitator reduces intervention; teams initiate repair moves
NATURALIZATION SIGN: repair language appears outside scheduled format
ARRIVAL CONDITION: repair becomes part of the regime, not an event supplied from outside
```

An agentic ecology aim:

```text
AIM: native task differentiation in a shared medium
TARGET: agents produce role-specialized behavior
CURRENT EXPRESSION: specialization appears under explicit role assignment
SUPPORTS: producer role table, prompt, external scheduler
MISSING CONDITION: basin differentiation through shared-medium traces and support gradients
WITHDRAWAL TEST: weaken explicit roles, preserve medium affordances
NATURALIZATION SIGN: differentiated continuations stabilize without role table control
ARRIVAL CONDITION: role basins persist as geometry rather than producer state
```

The ledger is not the development. It is a memory support for aim honesty. It helps the observer keep asking:

```text
Are we cultivating the condition, or chasing the sign?
```

## 11. Boundary and Transition to Organization

Aims of Reflexive Coherence Development does not replace Attunement of Becoming. It depends on it.

Attunement changed the unit of sight. It showed that loops become trajectories, trajectories become regimes, regimes interact as ecologies, and the observer is a scale-calibrated participant. It gave the language of integration and derivative: read the path, test the edge. It showed that identity is not a snapshot but a maintained basin under perturbation, and that coupling is not correlation but functional relation through support, boundary, substrate, or medium.

This paper has asked what aiming becomes inside that field.

A **target** is a local reference state.

An **objective** is a commitment to produce a defined result.

An **aim** is a directional commitment toward a condition regime.

An **orientation** is the current stance-specific vector of attention and action.

**Arrival** is entry into a condition envelope where the desired property can continue coherently.

**Development** is not movement toward a predefined end-state. It is the cultivation of coherent conditions under which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native.

**Condition-teleology** preserves direction without snapshot-finalism.

A **desired emergent property** is not merely an output. It is a property whose truth depends on function, support, variation, recovery, naturalization, scale, and effect on the surrounding field.

**Hope** is not magical optimism. It is orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence.

**Agency as an aim** means cultivating persistence, regeneration, transfer, support-generation, and generative participation under perturbation, not merely producing the signs of choice or independence.

**Redescription** is the disciplined update of an aim when the trajectory discloses that the original description is no longer faithful to the condition problem.

**Arrival without completion** means that the aim can be inhabited without being frozen.

The shortest formulation is:

```text
A target is reached.
An aim is cultivated.
Arrival is inhabited.
Hope orients.
Redescription keeps the orientation honest.
```

This paper also prepares the next shift.

Once aims become condition-oriented, organization cannot remain ordinary task execution toward predefined endpoints. Tasks still matter. Objectives still matter. Local completion still matters. But they must be reorganized under a larger question:

```text
What arrangement of activity, support, boundary, rhythm, role, shared medium,
withdrawal, and integration would allow the desired emergent property to become native?
```

If the aim is a condition regime, then activity must be organized as condition formation.

That is the work of the next paper.

The full movement so far is:

```text
Attunement changes sight.
Aims change direction.
```

The next movement follows naturally:

```text
Organization must change practice.
```
