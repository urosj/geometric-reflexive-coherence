This reference guide is a lookup artifact for **Reflexive Coherence Development**.

It is not another paper in the series. It does not try to re-argue the six-paper spiral. Its role is to stabilize the vocabulary, distinctions, ladders, operators, ledgers, and failure modes that practitioners need when applying the work.

Use this guide when you need to answer questions such as:

- What does this term mean in Reflexive Coherence Development?
- Which distinction prevents this claim from inflating?
- Which ladder should be used here?
- What is the difference between a sign, a function, a capacity, and a native capacity?
- What is the appropriate claim ceiling?
- What kind of support or scaffold is present?
- Is this a metric, a proxy, a support, a capacity, a regime, or an artifact?
- Which failure mode might be active?
- Which paper develops this idea in full?

The **User's Guide** is for starting practice.  
This **Reference Guide** is for keeping the practice precise.  
The future **Agentic Guide** can use this reference as the stable vocabulary for conversational facilitation.

# 1. Core Map

## 1.1 Domain Boundary

Objective-driven and target-state methods remain powerful when the world is sufficiently predefined:

- the target is known;
- the relevant states are stable;
- metrics reliably track the intended function;
- roles and responsibilities are established;
- outputs can be specified in advance;
- completion is the correct unit of evaluation.

Reflexive Coherence Development addresses the gap that appears when those conditions are not true:

- the target space itself is unfinished;
- the relevant classes may emerge during the work;
- the observer is embedded in the field being developed;
- desired properties may appear only under support;
- success signs may diverge from native capacity;
- aims may need redescription;
- the work must cultivate conditions rather than merely produce outputs.

This is not a rejection of predefined-world methods. It is a domain extension.

## 1.2 Core Definition

**Reflexive Coherence Development** is the discipline of cultivating coherent conditions under which desired emergent properties, identities, agencies, practices, and regimes can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native rather than merely scaffolded, performed, enforced, or wished for.

Compact form:

```text
Development is condition formation.
Arrival is entry into a condition regime.
Evidence is claim-ceiling discipline.
Stewardship is care of the conditions that make renewed becoming possible.
```

## 1.3 The Spiral in One Page

```text
1. ATTUNE
   What field am I inside?
   What has changed?
   What scale am I observing from?
   What trajectory is already moving?

2. AIM
   What desired emergent property are we orienting toward?
   What would arrival-as-condition mean?
   What hope is being made testable?

3. ORGANIZE
   What supports, boundaries, roles, rhythms, and shared media are needed?
   What cultivation move should be made next?
   What naturalization debt remains?

4. MEASURE
   What signs are visible?
   What function do they express?
   What support was required?
   What survives variation, withdrawal, transfer, and time?
   What claim ceiling is valid?

5. STEWARD
   What condition now needs care?
   What support should be maintained, weakened, repaired, or released?
   What agency is being cultivated, and at what cost?

6. RE-ATTUNE
   How did care change the field?
   What is now visible, fragile, blocked, native, or possible?
   What is the next beginning?
```

# 2. Core Distinctions

## 2.1 Target vs Aim

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Target** | A local reference, milestone, output, or probe endpoint. Useful when bounded and not mistaken for the whole developmental object. |
| **Aim** | A directional commitment toward a condition regime in which desired emergent properties can become native. |

A target may be part of an aim. It is not the same as an aim.

## 2.2 State vs Condition

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **State** | A configuration at a moment or under a defined measurement frame. |
| **Condition** | The boundary, support, rhythm, medium, agency, and regime structure that makes states possible, repeatable, recoverable, and revisable. |

A state can indicate a condition. It does not by itself prove the condition.

## 2.3 Output vs Emergent Property

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Output** | Produced result under given inputs, supports, and constraints. |
| **Emergent property** | A property that arises from the organization of conditions and may not be reducible to a single command, task, or component. |

Outputs can be produced. Emergent properties must be conditioned.

## 2.4 Sign vs Function vs Capacity vs Regime

| Level | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign** | Something visible happened. | The team held a retrospective. |
| **Function** | What the sign is supposed to express. | The team can reflect and adapt. |
| **Capacity** | The function can recur across variation. | Reflection still happens when the agenda changes. |
| **Native capacity** | The system generates the preconditions itself. | The team initiates reflection without management prompting. |
| **Regime** | Conditions sustain the capacity over time. | Reflection is embedded in rhythm, role, medium, trust, and decision structure. |

Most overclaims happen by jumping too quickly from sign to capacity or from supported capacity to native capacity.

## 2.5 Scaffolded vs Native

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scaffolded expression** | A property appears because an external support supplies part of the missing structure. |
| **Native expression** | A property appears because the system can generate the relevant preconditions from within its own regime. |

Scaffolded expression is valuable. It reveals possibility. But it is not yet native capacity.

## 2.6 Completion vs Arrival

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Completion** | A predefined task, output, or criterion has been satisfied. |
| **Arrival** | The system has entered a condition regime in which a desired emergent property can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, or be released without constant external forcing. |

Reflexive Coherence Development preserves arrival. It rejects only the reduction of arrival to terminal snapshot matching.

## 2.7 Planning vs Organization

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Planning** | Decomposing known objectives into actions, roles, timelines, and resources. |
| **Organization** | Arranging supports, boundaries, roles, rhythms, shared media, reserves, probes, withdrawals, and integration surfaces so desired properties can become native. |

Planning remains useful inside bounded zones. Organization becomes necessary when condition formation is the object.

## 2.8 Metric vs Measure

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Metric** | A specific indicator, score, count, or quantitative representation. |
| **Measure** | A disciplined evidence practice that asks what a sign supports, under what conditions, with what claim ceiling, and with which stronger claims still blocked. |

A metric can be part of a measure. It should not replace the measure.

## 2.9 Control vs Stewardship

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Control** | Directing outcomes or maintaining desired states through intervention. |
| **Stewardship** | Caring for conditions that allow coherent becoming, native capacity, agency, redescription, and renewal to continue. |

Stewardship may include control-like moves, but it is not reducible to control.

## 2.10 Repetition vs Renewal

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Repetition** | Running the same steps again as if the field were unchanged. |
| **Renewal** | Returning to attunement after the field has changed, carrying the previous cycle as history. |

The spiral returns, but it does not reset.

# 3. Term Reference

Each term is given in a compact operational form.

## Activity History

**Definition:** The preserved record of observations, classifications, probes, supports, withdrawals, measures, stewardship decisions, redescription events, and renewed attunements across cycles.

**Use:** Prevents loops from becoming isolated episodes.

**Not to be confused with:** A narrative summary. Activity history should preserve enough raw sequence to resist smoothing.

**Related:** trajectory, read-back, ledger, renewed attunement.

**Primary papers:** Attunement; The Spiral.

## Agency

**Definition:** Persistence, regeneration, transfer, support-generation, and participation under perturbation.

**Use:** Describes one of the main capacity-forms Reflexive Coherence Development may cultivate.

**Not to be confused with:** Conscious choice, isolated decision-making, intentionality, or goal declaration.

**Related:** native agency, scaffolded agency, proxy agency, wounded agency, extractive agency, generative agency.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Stewardship; Measures.

## Aim

**Definition:** A directional commitment toward a condition regime in which desired emergent properties can become native.

**Use:** Preserves direction without freezing the future into a predefined end-state.

**Not to be confused with:** Target, task, metric, wish, or prediction.

**Related:** orientation, hope, arrival, condition aim.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Arrival

**Definition:** Entry into a condition regime in which a desired emergent property can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, or be released without constant external forcing.

**Use:** Names meaningful arrival without requiring terminal configuration.

**Not to be confused with:** Completion, finality, success snapshot, permanent stability.

**Related:** aim, condition envelope, native capacity, stewardship.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Artifact

**Definition:** A written, computational, procedural, or symbolic object that preserves contact with the field.

**Use:** Ledgers, templates, measures, prompts, and maps can help sustain practice.

**Not to be confused with:** The practice itself. Artifact capture occurs when the artifact replaces contact.

**Related:** ledger, dashboard, artifact capture, measure.

**Primary papers:** Organization; Measures; The Spiral.

## Attunement

**Definition:** The discipline of reading development as continuous, path-dependent, scale-coupled, and observer-situated.

**Use:** Establishes where the observer is, what has accumulated, what scale is active, and what boundary is live before acting.

**Not to be confused with:** Passive observation, vague sensitivity, intuition alone, or restarting from zero.

**Related:** observer stance, trajectory, regime, ecology, renewed attunement.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Basin Function

**Definition:** A recurring role or pattern that stabilizes, routes, supports, bounds, integrates, or regenerates activity inside a regime.

**Use:** Reinterprets roles as functions in a support ecology rather than job labels alone.

**Not to be confused with:** Static responsibility assignment.

**Related:** role, support function, shared medium, organization.

**Primary paper:** Organization.

## Boundary

**Definition:** A condition of selective permeability: what can enter, exit, perturb, integrate, be filtered, or be resisted.

**Use:** Development often requires boundary disclosure, repair, strengthening, softening, or reconfiguration.

**Not to be confused with:** A wall, exclusion rule, or fixed edge.

**Related:** boundary elasticity, withdrawal, stewardship, derivative of identity.

**Primary papers:** Attunement; Organization; Stewardship.

## Boundary Elasticity

**Definition:** The ability of a boundary to stretch, absorb, route, or filter perturbation without collapse or rigid over-defense.

**Use:** Helps diagnose whether identity, capacity, regime, or organization can hold under variation.

**Not to be confused with:** Openness, compliance, tolerance, or lack of structure.

**Related:** derivative, withdrawal sensitivity, collapse threshold, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Attunement; Measures; Stewardship.

## Claim Ceiling

**Definition:** The strongest interpretation justified by the evidence under the supports, controls, variations, and withdrawals actually tested.

**Use:** Prevents signs from being promoted into unsupported claims.

**Not to be confused with:** Confidence, score, aspiration, or desired conclusion.

**Related:** blocked stronger claim, evidence, measure, naturalization.

**Primary paper:** Measures.

## Coherence Development

**Definition:** Short form for Reflexive Coherence Development: condition-oriented cultivation of coherent becoming.

**Use:** Names the practical track without always repeating the full title.

**Not to be confused with:** Generic self-improvement, optimization, or making things appear orderly.

**Related:** Reflexive Coherence Development, condition regime, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Entire spiral.

## Condition Aim

**Definition:** An aim stated as the formation of conditions rather than the production of a final state.

**Use:** Translates target-state language into developmental language.

**Not to be confused with:** A vague aspiration. A condition aim must name what would make the desired property more native.

**Related:** aim, desired emergent property, arrival condition, hope statement.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Condition Envelope

**Definition:** The range of conditions within which a desired property can arise, vary, recover, transfer, or be revised without losing coherence.

**Use:** Replaces a single target snapshot with a viable region.

**Not to be confused with:** Any acceptable outcome, loose tolerance, or unbounded openness.

**Related:** arrival, viability regime, native capacity, stewardship.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Condition Regime

**Definition:** A stabilized but revisable field of boundaries, supports, rhythms, shared media, roles, measures, agencies, and reserves that sustains developmental properties over time.

**Use:** Names the object of condition-oriented development.

**Not to be confused with:** A state, institution, policy, procedure, or equilibrium alone.

**Related:** regime, stewardship, arrival, support ecology.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Organization; Stewardship.

## Cultivation Move

**Definition:** A concrete activity that changes, tests, supports, withdraws, repairs, or integrates a condition.

**Use:** Replaces task as the primary unit of open-ended developmental practice.

**Not to be confused with:** Task completion. A cultivation move may produce no immediate output while still altering conditions.

**Examples:** disclose a boundary, weaken a scaffold, test transfer, restore a rhythm, repair a shared medium, reduce naturalization debt.

**Primary paper:** Organization.

## Dashboard

**Definition:** A display or system of indicators that can aid visibility but risks replacing contact with the field.

**Use:** Useful in predefined domains or as limited support, dangerous when treated as authority over developmental reality.

**Not to be confused with:** A measure, ledger, or evidence discipline.

**Related:** artifact capture, metric capture, claim ceiling.

**Primary papers:** Measures; The Spiral.

## Desired Emergent Property

**Definition:** A property the practitioner wants to make naturally possible, but which may require conditions rather than direct production.

**Use:** The central object of an aim.

**Not to be confused with:** Output, deliverable, preference, or metric.

**Examples:** agency, learning, regulation, coordination, resilience, abundance, trust, ecological recovery, native reasoning.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Development

**Definition:** The formation, stabilization, naturalization, revision, and stewardship of coherent conditions.

**Use:** Reframes development away from mere progress toward predefined snapshots.

**Not to be confused with:** Improvement, growth, optimization, completion, or upward motion.

**Related:** condition formation, trajectory morphology, arrival, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Aims; The Spiral.

## Developmental Update Operator

**Definition:** The notional map by which a completed loop is integrated into the system-observer field.

**Use:** Formal shorthand for the fact that loops change the next loop's starting condition.

**Notation:**

$$
M_{k+1} = \Phi(M_k, L_k)
$$

where `\Phi` is the developmental update operator.

**Not to be confused with:** A known deterministic function, algorithm, optimization rule, or externally controllable transition.

**Related:** loop trace, integration, read-back, trajectory.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Ecology

**Definition:** An interaction among regimes under shared substrate constraints, selection pressures, boundary porosity, and cross-regime resonance or dampening.

**Use:** Names the level at which multiple condition regimes influence each other.

**Not to be confused with:** Biological ecology only, or a larger organization by default.

**Related:** regime, scale coupling, shared medium, stewardship.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Endogenous Precondition

**Definition:** A support, boundary, rhythm, or function that the system can generate from within its own regime.

**Use:** Indicates movement toward native capacity.

**Not to be confused with:** A support that is still supplied externally but has become familiar.

**Related:** naturalization, native capacity, support withdrawal, N-ladder.

**Primary papers:** Naturalization background; Aims; Measures.

## Evidence

**Definition:** A sign or pattern interpreted within its support context, variation history, withdrawal behavior, comparator behavior, and claim ceiling.

**Use:** Prevents metric output from becoming unsupported proof.

**Not to be confused with:** Raw data, score, output, or anecdote alone.

**Related:** measure, claim ceiling, blocked stronger claim.

**Primary paper:** Measures.

## Function

**Definition:** What a sign, behavior, role, metric, or practice is supposed to express or accomplish in the developmental field.

**Use:** Keeps measurement and organization tied to the property being cultivated.

**Not to be confused with:** The visible sign of the function.

**Related:** sign, capacity, role, measure.

**Primary papers:** Organization; Measures.

## Hope

**Definition:** A disciplined orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergent properties.

**Use:** Makes hope descriptive, expressive, and actionable without reducing it to optimism.

**Not to be confused with:** Wishful thinking, prediction, fantasy, or denial of evidence.

**Related:** aim, condition hypothesis, hope ladder, redescription.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Identity

**Definition:** A maintained basin or coherent pattern that persists through flux, perturbation, repair, variation, and boundary response.

**Use:** Replaces identity-as-snapshot with identity-as-maintained continuity.

**Not to be confused with:** A fixed state, label, trait, or stored essence.

**Related:** derivative of identity, agency, boundary elasticity, basin.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Integration

**Definition:** The accumulation of developmental effects across loops, trajectories, regimes, and ecologies.

**Use:** Names how loops alter the future field rather than merely producing records.

**Not to be confused with:** Summarization, storage, agreement, or closure.

**Related:** trajectory, read-back, developmental update operator, renewed attunement.

**Primary papers:** Attunement; The Spiral.

## Ledger

**Definition:** A lightweight artifact that preserves contact with field history, supports, measures, claim ceilings, stewardship decisions, and renewed attunement.

**Use:** Helps prevent reset, narrative compression, and isolated-loop practice.

**Not to be confused with:** Dashboard, performance tracker, bureaucratic form, or proof.

**Related:** artifact capture, activity history, unified practice ledger.

**Primary papers:** Measures; The Spiral; User's Guide.

## Loop Trace

**Definition:** The record of what a cycle actually did: orientation, observation, classification, probe, support, withdrawal, naturalization attempt, integration, claim ceiling, and side-effects.

**Use:** Input to renewed attunement and trajectory reading.

**Notation:** Often denoted `L_k`.

**Not to be confused with:** The result alone.

**Related:** `M_k`, `\Phi`, activity history.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Measure

**Definition:** A disciplined evidence practice that asks what a sign supports, under what supports, across what variation, with what withdrawal behavior, and with which stronger claims blocked.

**Use:** Protects developmental practice from metric capture.

**Not to be confused with:** Metric, score, KPI, dashboard, or evaluation ritual.

**Related:** sign, function, claim ceiling, support context, comparator.

**Primary paper:** Measures.

## Metric Capture

**Definition:** A failure mode in which the metric becomes the object of development rather than a limited sign of a function.

**Use:** Diagnoses when practice optimizes visible indicators while degrading native capacity or regime health.

**Not to be confused with:** Legitimate metric use in predefined domains.

**Related:** proxy localization, sign/capacity confusion, dashboard illusion.

**Primary paper:** Measures.

## Native Capacity

**Definition:** A capacity whose relevant preconditions can be generated by the system's own regime rather than continuously supplied from outside.

**Use:** Central distinction for development, agency, measurement, and stewardship.

**Not to be confused with:** Repeated scaffolded performance.

**Related:** naturalization, support withdrawal, endogenous precondition, N-ladder.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Measures; Stewardship.

## Naturalization

**Definition:** The process by which a property moves from externally supported expression toward endogenous precondition generation and native recurrence.

**Use:** Distinguishes supported possibility from developed capacity.

**Not to be confused with:** Familiarity, repetition, automation, compliance, or habit alone.

**Related:** scaffold, native capacity, naturalization ladder, naturalization debt.

**Primary papers:** Naturalization background; Measures.

## Naturalization Debt

**Definition:** The remaining external scaffolds, explicit producer state, rules, prompts, supports, or controls that are still carrying a function that the system is intended to generate natively.

**Use:** Especially important in organization and agentic systems.

**Not to be confused with:** Bad scaffolding. Scaffolds can be useful; debt names what still has not become native.

**Related:** scaffold, producer state, support withdrawal, organization.

**Primary paper:** Organization.

## Observer Stance

**Definition:** The observer's active position in a field, defined by what they can resolve, perturb, withdraw, compare, integrate, and steward at a given scale.

**Use:** Prevents false externality and scale collapse.

**Not to be confused with:** Perspective, opinion, role title, or neutral viewpoint.

**Related:** attunement, scale, intervention bandwidth, claim ceiling.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Orientation

**Definition:** The current direction of attention and action inside the field.

**Use:** Guides the next cultivation move without pretending to define the final state.

**Not to be confused with:** Aim as a whole, target, plan, or prediction.

**Related:** aim, attunement, hope, next cultivation move.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Predefined Development

**Definition:** Development practice in domains where targets, outputs, roles, metrics, and success criteria are sufficiently specified in advance.

**Use:** Names the domain where objective-driven methods are powerful.

**Not to be confused with:** A wrong or inferior method. It is a local regime with valid applicability.

**Related:** target-state method, domain boundary, condition-oriented development.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Organization; Measures.

## Proxy Localization

**Definition:** A failure mode in which the practitioner begins cultivating a local proxy rather than the function or condition the proxy was meant to indicate.

**Use:** Diagnoses developmental drift into indicator optimization.

**Not to be confused with:** Using local proxies carefully under explicit claim ceilings.

**Related:** metric capture, naturalization debt, sign/capacity confusion.

**Primary papers:** Measures; Stewardship.

## Redescription

**Definition:** The disciplined reformulation of an aim, hope, measure, or stewardship stance when evidence shows the previous description no longer holds.

**Use:** Allows commitment to remain honest without collapsing into failure or denial.

**Not to be confused with:** Giving up, rationalization, rebranding, or goal drift.

**Related:** hope, blocked claims, grief, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Stewardship.

## Regime

**Definition:** A condition-field that sustains trajectories, supports, boundaries, rhythms, shared media, roles, agencies, and measures over time.

**Use:** Names the level into which trajectories integrate.

**Not to be confused with:** Static system, organization, institution, equilibrium, or state.

**Related:** condition regime, stewardship, ecology, trajectory.

**Primary papers:** Attunement; Stewardship.

## Renewed Attunement

**Definition:** The return to attunement after stewardship has changed the field.

**Use:** Prevents the spiral from becoming a linear sequence or reset.

**Not to be confused with:** Starting over, repeating the method, or abandoning the previous cycle.

**Related:** spiral, activity history, read-back, field change.

**Primary paper:** The Spiral.

## Rhythm

**Definition:** A recurring temporal pattern that allows exploration, consolidation, withdrawal, repair, synthesis, rest, and renewal.

**Use:** Organization depends on rhythm because conditions form across time, not only through tasks.

**Not to be confused with:** Schedule alone.

**Related:** cadence, consolidation window, withdrawal interval, spiral.

**Primary papers:** Organization; The Spiral.

## Scaffold

**Definition:** External support that enables a property to appear before the system can generate the relevant preconditions natively.

**Use:** Reveals possibility and supports cultivation.

**Not to be confused with:** Native capacity, manipulation, or invalid evidence. Scaffolded expression is valid at the proper claim ceiling.

**Related:** support, naturalization, withdrawal, naturalization debt.

**Primary papers:** Aims; Measures; Stewardship.

## Shared Medium

**Definition:** The field through which coordination, trace, susceptibility, pressure, reserve, timing, and co-response are carried.

**Use:** Explains why organization is not only message passing between roles or agents.

**Not to be confused with:** Communication channel alone.

**Related:** organization, agentic ecology, medium health, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Organization; Stewardship.

## Sign

**Definition:** A visible indicator that something appeared.

**Use:** Starting point for evidence.

**Not to be confused with:** Function, capacity, native capacity, or regime.

**Related:** metric, measure, evidence ladder, claim ceiling.

**Primary paper:** Measures.

## Spiral

**Definition:** The recurring developmental rhythm in which each cycle returns to attunement after changing the field.

**Use:** Prevents the series from being read as a ladder of completion.

**Not to be confused with:** Circular repetition, infinite process overhead, or ritualized method.

**Related:** renewed attunement, stewardship, cadence, habitat.

**Primary paper:** The Spiral.

## Stewardship

**Definition:** Responsible care of the conditions under which coherent becoming, native capacity, agency, redescription, and renewal can continue.

**Use:** Names mature developmental care once regimes begin to exist or require protection.

**Not to be confused with:** Control, preservation, maintenance, paternalism, or abandonment.

**Related:** boundary care, support care, agency care, release, renewed attunement.

**Primary paper:** Stewardship.

## Support Ecology

**Definition:** The network of supports, roles, boundaries, reserves, rhythms, media, practices, and scaffolds that make a property possible.

**Use:** Identifies what carries a function before it becomes native.

**Not to be confused with:** Resources alone.

**Related:** naturalization, organization, stewardship, hidden support.

**Primary papers:** Organization; Stewardship.

## Target

**Definition:** A bounded endpoint, result, metric, or configuration used for local orientation or completion.

**Use:** Useful when treated as local and revisable.

**Not to be confused with:** Aim, arrival, regime, or development itself.

**Related:** predefined development, aim, target-state capture.

**Primary paper:** Aims.

## Trajectory

**Definition:** Path-dependent deformation of a developmental field across loops.

**Use:** Makes continuity first-class.

**Not to be confused with:** A line of snapshots, narrative progress story, or fixed path.

**Related:** morphology, integration, activity history, regime.

**Primary paper:** Attunement.

## Withdrawal

**Definition:** The disciplined weakening, removal, variation, or substitution of support to test whether a property is scaffolded or native.

**Use:** One of the strongest truth-tests in Reflexive Coherence Development.

**Not to be confused with:** Abandonment, neglect, punishment, or proof by deprivation.

**Related:** naturalization, support decay curve, claim ceiling, stewardship.

**Primary papers:** Measures; Stewardship.

# 4. Ladders, Scales, and Morphologies

These structures are diagnostic aids. They are not progress bars unless explicitly stated otherwise. Most are **claim disciplines**, not optimization targets.

## 4.1 Six Grounds of Reflexive Coherence Development

| Ground | Question | What it changes | Output of the ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attunement** | What field am I inside? | Sight | Field/trajectory/stance read |
| **Aims** | What should become possible? | Direction | Condition aim and arrival condition |
| **Organization** | What conditions must be arranged? | Practice | Cultivation moves, roles, rhythms, supports |
| **Measures** | What can be honestly claimed? | Evidence | Claim ceiling and measurement plan |
| **Stewardship** | What needs care, withdrawal, repair, or release? | Care | Stewardship stance and regime responsibility |
| **Renewed Attunement** | How did care change the field? | Return | Next beginning |

## 4.2 Claim Ceiling Ladder

Use this ladder when deciding how strong a claim the evidence supports.

| Rung | Claim type | Example form | Common overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C0 Sign/Event** | Something appeared. | `The behavior occurred.` | Treating occurrence as function. |
| **C1 Consequence/Function** | The sign had a functional consequence. | `The behavior supported coordination.` | Treating consequence as stable capacity. |
| **C2 Geometry/Condition** | A condition or boundary pattern is implicated. | `Coordination depends on this rhythm and shared trace.` | Treating condition as specificity. |
| **C3 Specificity** | The effect is specific rather than generic or shared substrate noise. | `This support matters for this regime, not all controls.` | Treating specificity as recurrence. |
| **C4 Recurrence/Transfer** | The function recurs across variation or context. | `Coordination appears across modified meetings and teams.` | Treating recurrence as native expression. |
| **C5 Natural Expression** | The system generates the relevant preconditions itself. | `The team initiates and sustains coordination without external prompting.` | Treating native expression as permanent maturity. |
| **C6 Self-Interrogating Regime** | The system generates its own probe/support function. | `The regime notices and repairs its own coordination conditions.` | Treating self-interrogation as immunity from collapse. |

Always state the blocked stronger claim.

## 4.3 Naturalization Ladder

Use this ladder when distinguishing scaffolded possibility from native capacity.

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **N0** | Probe-dependent | Property appears only under direct probe/support. |
| **N1** | Weakened support | Property holds under slightly reduced support. |
| **N2** | Regime-assisted | Existing regime conditions help carry the property, but support remains external or structured. |
| **N3** | Endogenous precondition | The system begins generating some preconditions internally. |
| **N4** | Native expression | The property appears through the system's own condition regime. |
| **N5** | Recurrent native expression | Native expression recurs across variation, time, or neighboring contexts. |
| **N6** | Self-interrogating regime | The system generates the boundary/probe/support function needed to continue developing the property. |

N-levels can move downward. Naturalization is not monotonic.

## 4.4 Hope Ladder

Use this ladder when clarifying whether hope is wishful, oriented, testable, grounded, or naturalized.

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **H0** | Wish | Desired property is imagined, but no condition model exists. |
| **H1** | Oriented hope | Desired property is named as an aim. |
| **H2** | Condition hypothesis | Missing supports, boundaries, rhythms, or media are identified. |
| **H3** | Scaffolded hope | Property appears under supplied conditions. |
| **H4** | Tested hope | Property partially survives variation or support weakening. |
| **H5** | Grounded hope | The system begins generating some preconditions internally. |
| **H6** | Naturalized hope | Desired property can recur through endogenous conditions. |
| **H7** | Ecological hope | The regime supports multiple future goods, not only one desired property. |

Hope can be redescribed without being destroyed.

## 4.5 Evidence Ladder

Use this ladder when interpreting metrics, observations, or reports.

| Level | Evidence asks | Claim allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **E0 Data/Sign** | What appeared? | Occurrence claim. |
| **E1 Function** | What did it do? | Functional consequence claim. |
| **E2 Support context** | What carried it? | Scaffold/support claim. |
| **E3 Variation** | Does it hold when conditions change? | Robustness claim. |
| **E4 Withdrawal** | Does it survive reduced support? | Dependency/naturalization claim. |
| **E5 Transfer** | Does it appear in neighboring contexts? | Transferability claim. |
| **E6 Regime** | Are conditions sustaining it? | Regime claim. |

A score usually enters at E0 or E1. It does not automatically support higher rungs.

## 4.6 Agency Forms

These are not moral rankings. They describe how agency persists and what it costs.

| Form | Meaning | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| **Scaffolded agency** | Agency-like behavior depends on external support. | What must be supplied for it to persist? |
| **Native agency** | The pattern generates its own preconditions. | What support can be withdrawn without collapse? |
| **Transferable agency** | Agency persists across context/substrate variation. | Where else can it carry itself? |
| **Proxy agency** | Agency-like behavior optimizes a substitute sign. | What function has the proxy replaced? |
| **Wounded agency** | Agency persists through defensive contraction or narrowed range. | What perturbations force rigidity? |
| **Extractive agency** | Agency persists by weakening the surrounding field. | What does its persistence cost others or the medium? |
| **Generative agency** | Agency increases agency-capacity in the surrounding field. | What becomes more possible around it? |

## 4.7 Trajectory Morphologies

Use this when reading several loops together.

| Morphology | Signature | Not automatically |
|---|---|---|
| **Compounding spiral** | Resonance density increases, support dependence decreases, claim ceilings rise. | Good; may be proxy optimization. |
| **Equilibrating/circular spiral** | Stable recurrence, maintenance, low change. | Stagnation; may be healthy homeostasis. |
| **Diminishing spiral** | Support dependence rises, latency increases, claim ceilings fall. | Failure; may be needed withdrawal or redescription. |
| **Bifurcating trajectory** | A new class, aim, regime, or path separates from the prior pattern. | Chaos; may be emergence. |
| **Fragmenting trajectory** | Integration fails, signs scatter, supports conflict, identity/path coherence weakens. | Necessary collapse; may require repair or release. |
| **Dormant trajectory** | Low visible motion but preserved latent support or reserve. | Absence; may be consolidation. |

Morphology is descriptive. It is not a target.

## 4.8 Observer Stance Forms

Stances are not titles. They are operational positions revealed by what the observer can do.

| Stance | Primary question | Typical work |
|---|---|---|
| **Classifier** | What appeared? | Name signs at the lowest valid rung. |
| **Interrogator** | Which boundary is missing? | Probe, compare, and bound claims. |
| **Cultivator** | What condition would help this become? | Supply supports and test naturalization. |
| **Trajectory mapper** | What is accumulating across loops? | Read morphology, latency, support curves. |
| **Regime steward** | What conditions sustain paths? | Care for boundaries, supports, rhythms, media. |
| **Ecology participant** | How do regimes interact? | Attend to cross-regime coupling and substrate constraints. |
| **Renewed attuner** | How did care change the field? | Re-enter with history, not reset. |

# 5. Operators and Formal Notation

The formal language in Reflexive Coherence Development is used to strengthen distinctions, not to make the framework appear more scientific than it is. Unless explicitly specified, these operators are **not assumed to be known, linear, deterministic, or closed-form**.

## 5.1 Developmental Condition

Let:

$$
M_k
$$

name the developmental condition of the system-observer field at cycle `k`.

`M_k` is not a snapshot of state. It includes, at minimum:

- basin structure;
- boundary elasticity;
- support dependence;
- class vocabulary;
- claim ceilings;
- activity history;
- trajectory morphology;
- observer stance;
- shared-medium condition;
- stewardship decisions and residual support debt.

## 5.2 Loop Trace

Let:

$$
L_k
$$

name the trace of cycle `k`.

A loop trace may include:

$$
L_k = (O_k, A_k, C_k, P_k, W_k, N_k, I_k)
$$

where, depending on context:

- `O_k` = orientation/observation;
- `A_k` = aim or local orientation vector;
- `C_k` = classification or claim ceiling;
- `P_k` = probe/support introduced;
- `W_k` = withdrawal/variation result;
- `N_k` = naturalization status;
- `I_k` = integration record.

This tuple is schematic. It names what must be preserved, not a mandatory data schema.

## 5.3 Developmental Update Operator

A completed loop alters the next loop's starting condition:

$$
M_{k+1} = \Phi(M_k, L_k)
$$

where `\Phi` is the **developmental update operator**: the notional map by which a completed loop is integrated into the system-observer field. It does not represent a known deterministic function or externally controlled transition rule. It names the irreducible operation through which orientation, classification, probing, withdrawal, naturalization, and integration alter the condition of the field itself.

Formally,

$$
\Phi : \mathcal{M} \times \mathcal{L} \to \mathcal{M}
$$

where `\mathcal{M}` is the space of developmental conditions and `\mathcal{L}` is the space of loop traces. In practical terms, `\Phi` includes changes in basin depth, boundary elasticity, support dependence, class vocabulary, read-back flux, claim ceilings, shared-medium condition, and observer stance. It is inferred from what the next loop begins with: what has become easier to notice, harder to sustain, newly possible, newly blocked, more native, or more dependent.

## 5.4 Integration

Integration is the accumulation operator of the practice.

Informally:

$$
\text{Trajectory}_{a:b} = \int_a^b dM
$$

This does not claim that `M` is necessarily differentiable or numerically measurable. It names the discipline of reading the accumulated deformation of the field across cycles.

Operationally, integration asks:

```text
What changed across loops?
What became native?
What became more dependent?
What class vocabulary was promoted or retired?
What support debt accumulated?
What trajectory morphology is visible?
What did the observer learn to see?
```

## 5.5 Derivative of Identity

The derivative of identity names boundary sensitivity and flux responsiveness.

Informally:

$$
D_I(p) \sim \frac{\Delta I}{\Delta p}
$$

where `p` is a perturbation, support change, withdrawal, or variation. `D_I(p)` is not usually a scalar. It is a response profile.

A response profile may include:

- latency to recovery;
- boundary elasticity;
- support dependence;
- collapse threshold;
- bifurcation tendency;
- integration route;
- deflection route;
- claim ceiling shift.

Practical question:

```text
When this identity or capacity is perturbed, does it stretch, integrate, deflect,
collapse, bifurcate, rigidify, or regenerate?
```

## 5.6 Support Decay Curve

A support decay curve tracks what happens as external support is weakened.

Let `s` denote support intensity. A property `Q` may have expression strength:

$$
Q = Q(s)
$$

The diagnostic is not simply whether `Q` decreases. The diagnostic is how it decreases:

- sharp collapse = high scaffold dependence;
- gradual reduction = partial internal support;
- stable expression = possible native capacity;
- transformation into another form = redescription or bifurcation;
- improved expression under lower support = over-scaffolding may have been suppressing native dynamics.

## 5.7 Claim Ceiling Function

Let:

$$
\mathrm{ceil}(E, S, V, W, K)
$$

name the strongest claim supported by evidence `E`, support context `S`, variation `V`, withdrawal behavior `W`, and comparator/control context `K`.

The output is not a number by default. It is a rung:

```text
sign/event → function/consequence → condition/geometry → specificity →
recurrence/transfer → native expression → self-interrogating regime
```

## 5.8 Coupling vs Correlation

Correlation asks whether two patterns co-vary.

Coupling asks whether one regime supplies, constrains, filters, or alters the boundary/support conditions of another.

A coupling claim requires some combination of:

- substitution test;
- withdrawal curve;
- comparator divergence;
- integration latency;
- claim ceiling shift;
- specificity across controls.

Without these, the safer claim is co-variation, not coupling.

## 5.9 Redescription

Redescription is an update to the aim or interpretation when evidence blocks the prior formulation.

In notation:

$$
A_{k+1} = R(A_k, E_k, B_k)
$$

where:

- `A_k` = current aim;
- `E_k` = evidence and measures from the field;
- `B_k` = blocked stronger claims;
- `R` = redescription operation.

This is not failure by default. It is how hope and aim remain honest.

## 5.10 Renewed Attunement

Renewed attunement is the next field-read after stewardship.

If stewardship action `S_k` alters the field:

$$
M_{k+1} = \Psi(M_k, S_k)
$$

then renewed attunement asks:

$$
A^{\mathrm{attune}}_{k+1} = \mathcal{R}(M_{k+1}, H_k)
$$

where `H_k` is the inherited activity history. This notation is schematic. Its purpose is to show that the return is not reset. It is re-entry with history.

# 7. Failure Modes

Use this section diagnostically. A failure mode is not a condemnation. It is a signal that a distinction has collapsed or a support condition has become hidden.

## 7.1 Quick Failure Mode Table

| Failure mode | How it appears | What it confuses | Corrective move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Isolated-loop practice** | Each cycle produces notes but no trajectory. | Integration with storage. | Read across loops; update trajectory morphology. |
| **Target-state capture** | The aim freezes into a desired snapshot. | Aim with target. | Rewrite as condition aim and arrival condition. |
| **Objective overextension** | Predefined-world methods are used where classes/supports are still emerging. | Stable target with unfinished target space. | Name domain boundary; use condition formation. |
| **Snapshot identity** | Identity is judged by momentary configuration. | State with maintained basin. | Probe boundary response and recovery. |
| **Narrative compression** | History is smoothed into a progress story. | Trajectory with story. | Preserve raw sequence and blocked claims. |
| **Progress bias** | Compounding is assumed to be good. | Growth with development. | Read morphology descriptively. |
| **Scale collapse** | Micro evidence is treated as macro proof. | Local effect with regime/ecology claim. | State claim ceiling by scale. |
| **Correlation collapse** | Co-variation is treated as coupling. | Correlation with functional dependency. | Use substitution, withdrawal, comparator divergence. |
| **Scaffold/native confusion** | Supported expression is called capacity. | Possibility with naturalization. | Test withdrawal and endogenous preconditions. |
| **Hidden support** | Capacity disappears when an unacknowledged actor, artifact, or rhythm is removed. | System capacity with support ecology. | Map supports explicitly. |
| **Naturalization debt denial** | Explicit scaffolds are treated as permanent design. | Useful support with native condition. | Track debt and define withdrawal/substitution path. |
| **Metric capture** | The metric becomes the goal. | Sign with function. | Reconnect metric to function and claim ceiling. |
| **Proxy localization** | Practice optimizes a local proxy. | Proxy with property. | Re-state desired emergent property. |
| **Artifact capture** | Ledger/dashboard replaces contact. | Artifact with practice. | Reduce artifact; return to field questions. |
| **Spiral fatigue** | The practice becomes mechanical. | Rhythm with repetition. | Lengthen cadence, reduce overhead, re-attune to medium. |
| **Cadence compression** | Phases blur under urgency. | Speed with development. | Separate phase boundaries and reset claim ceilings. |
| **Stewardship rigidity** | Care protects current form against needed change. | Stewardship with preservation. | Ask what the regime is preventing. |
| **Abandonment as withdrawal** | Support is removed without care. | Withdrawal test with neglect. | Define safe weakening and repair conditions. |
| **Overprotection** | Support prevents native capacity from forming. | Care with control. | Test gradual support decay. |
| **Extractive agency** | A pattern persists by weakening the field. | Agency with good agency. | Measure agency cost and surrounding capacity. |
| **Redescription avoidance** | The aim remains fixed despite blocked evidence. | Commitment with honesty. | Redescribe aim and hope. |
| **Hope fantasy** | Hope has no condition hypothesis. | Desire with grounded hope. | Name missing supports/boundaries. |
| **Habitat illusion** | Familiar routine is treated as naturalization. | Familiarity with native capacity. | Use withdrawal, transfer, and medium health checks. |
| **Role ossification** | Roles become identity claims rather than support functions. | Role label with basin function. | Re-map roles by function. |
| **Shared-medium degradation** | Coordination worsens despite individual performance. | Message passing with medium health. | Repair trace, reserve, timing, trust, susceptibility. |

## 7.2 Diagnostic Questions by Failure Class

### If the work feels stuck

```text
Are we reading trajectory or only current state?
Are we mistaking equilibrium for stagnation?
Are we protecting a form that should be redescribed?
Is a hidden support carrying the function?
```

### If the work feels successful but fragile

```text
What support is still external?
What happens under variation?
What happens under withdrawal?
What stronger claim remains blocked?
```

### If the work feels bureaucratic

```text
Has the artifact replaced contact?
Is the ledger longer than the field requires?
Are phases being performed because they are named?
What is the smallest artifact that preserves honesty?
```

### If hope feels unrealistic

```text
Is there a condition hypothesis?
What missing boundary, support, rhythm, or medium would make the property possible?
Can we test a scaffolded expression without claiming native capacity?
Does the aim need redescription?
```

### If measurement feels misleading

```text
What is the sign?
What function is the sign supposed to indicate?
What support was present?
What variation, withdrawal, transfer, or comparator has been tested?
What claim ceiling is valid?
```

# 9. Common Reference Questions

## 9.1 Is this a target or an aim?

Ask:

```text
Can this be satisfied by producing a configuration once?
```

If yes, it may be a target.

Ask:

```text
Does this require conditions under which a property can arise, stabilize, vary,
recover, transfer, or become native?
```

If yes, it is probably an aim.

## 9.2 Is this evidence of native capacity?

Ask:

```text
What support was present?
What variation was tested?
What happened under withdrawal?
Did the system generate any preconditions itself?
Did the property transfer?
What stronger claim remains blocked?
```

Without withdrawal or endogenous precondition evidence, the safer claim is scaffolded expression or supported capacity.

## 9.3 Is this hope grounded?

Ask:

```text
Has the desired property been named?
Has a condition hypothesis been formed?
Has scaffolded expression appeared?
Has any support been weakened?
Has any precondition become endogenous?
```

Hope becomes grounded as conditions become testable and partially native.

## 9.4 Is this organization or task execution?

Ask:

```text
Are we only assigning work toward outputs?
Or are we arranging supports, boundaries, rhythms, roles, media, reserves,
withdrawals, and integration surfaces?
```

Task execution may be part of organization, but condition-oriented organization asks what the task changes in the field.

## 9.5 Is this stewardship or control?

Ask:

```text
Are we preserving the current form because it is familiar?
Are we caring for the conditions that allow coherent becoming to continue?
Are we willing to weaken support, release form, or redescribe aim when needed?
```

Stewardship protects becoming, not merely the present regime.

## 9.6 Is the spiral returning or just repeating?

Ask:

```text
What changed because of the previous cycle?
What does the next cycle inherit?
What is now visible that was not visible before?
What claim ceiling shifted?
What support debt remains?
```

If the next cycle begins as if nothing changed, the spiral has become repetition.

# 12. Minimal Glossary

For quick recall:

```text
Aim
  Direction toward a condition regime, not a frozen future state.

  Entering conditions where desired emergence can recur, vary, recover, and become native.

  The strongest interpretation supported by tested evidence and support context.

  What should become naturally possible.

  Orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence.

Measure
  Evidence discipline that protects signs from overclaim.

Naturalization
  Movement from scaffolded expression toward endogenous precondition generation.

Organization
  Arrangement of supports, boundaries, roles, rhythms, media, and cultivation moves.

Renewed attunement
  Returning to sight after care has changed the field.

Scaffold
  External support that enables a property before it is native.

Trajectory
  Path-dependent field deformation across loops.

  Weakening or varying support to test dependency and naturalization.
```

# 13. Closing Reference Rule

When uncertain, return to the smallest honest distinction:

```text
What appeared?
What function might it express?
What support carried it?
What survives variation or withdrawal?
What can be claimed now?
What stronger claim remains blocked?
What condition should be cultivated next?
```

This rule is the practical heart of the reference guide. It preserves development as contact with the field rather than movement toward an imagined snapshot.
