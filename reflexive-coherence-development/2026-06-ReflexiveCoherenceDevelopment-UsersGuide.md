# Reflexive Coherence Development: A User's Guide
## A Short Field Manual for Condition-Oriented Practice

**Date:** 2026-06-26

Copyright © 2026 Uroš Jovanovič, CC BY-SA 4.0.

## How to Use This Guide

This guide is a practical entry into Reflexive Coherence Development.

It is not a replacement for the six papers. It is a field manual: a small set of questions, ledgers, distinctions, and practice rhythms that allow someone to begin applying the work before they have fully internalized the whole framework.

Use it when the desired result is not simply a predefined deliverable, but an emergent property that must become more native over time.

For example:

- not only `finish the training`, but cultivate learning that transfers;
- not only `enforce the process`, but form coordination that can regenerate locally;
- not only `make the agent answer correctly`, but distinguish scaffolded performance from native capability;
- not only `make the person calm`, but cultivate conditions under which regulation can return after perturbation;
- not only `restore the site`, but form ecological conditions that can sustain recovery without constant intervention.

Target-state and objective-driven methods remain powerful when the world is sufficiently predefined: when the target is known, the states are stable, the metrics reliably track the function, the roles are established, and completion is the right unit of evaluation.

Reflexive Coherence Development is for the gap that appears when the target space itself is unfinished: when the relevant classes may emerge during the work, the observer is part of the field, the desired property may appear only under support, and development means forming conditions rather than merely producing outputs.

The six papers behind this guide form a spiral:

```text
Attunement changes sight.
Aims change direction.
Organization changes practice.
Measures change evidence.
Stewardship changes care.
Care changes the field.
The changed field requires renewed attunement.
```

The guide follows the same rhythm.


A cycle can take five minutes, a week, a research season, a therapy phase, an organizational quarter, or the lifetime of an institution. The scale changes. The discipline does not.

# 2. Minimum Viable Practice

Start here if you want to apply the framework immediately.

Choose one living concern. Do not choose a vague life theme. Choose something active enough that a next move is possible.

Examples:

```text
A team cannot coordinate without a manager resolving every boundary.
A research project keeps generating observations but no stable class vocabulary.
An AI system performs well under prompts but collapses when the scaffold changes.
A person can regulate in session but not after real-world perturbation.
A community practice works during events but does not persist between them.
```

Then fill this in:

```text
1. Field
What system, practice, relationship, organization, model, ecology, or trajectory am I inside?

2. Desired emergent property
What do I want to become naturally possible here?

3. Current expression
Where does this property already appear, even weakly, partially, or under support?

4. External supports
What currently has to be supplied from outside for it to appear?

5. Condition hypothesis
What boundary, rhythm, support, role, medium, reserve, or practice might let it become more native?

6. Next cultivation move
What small action will test or improve the condition without pretending to prove the whole aim?
```

This is the minimum practice.

The important move is from output to condition.

Do not ask only:

```text
How do we get the desired result?
```

Ask:

```text
What conditions would allow the desired property to arise, stabilize, vary, recover,
and eventually become less dependent on external support?
```

# 3. Working with Existing Objectives

Most real work begins with an objective already in place.

The guide does not ask you to reject it. Instead, translate it.

An objective can remain useful as a local reference, constraint, deadline, or deliverable. The shift is that it no longer carries the full meaning of development.

Use this translation:

| Given objective | Reflexive Coherence Development question |
| --- | --- |
| Deliver X | What condition should delivering X help form? |
| Improve metric Y | What function is Y supposed to indicate? |
| Make behavior Z happen | What support currently makes Z possible? |
| Train capacity C | What would show that C is becoming native? |
| Implement process P | What rhythm, boundary, or medium should P stabilize? |
| Complete project Q | What should be different about the field after Q? |

A predefined objective can become one of three things:

1. **A constraint**: something the work must respect.
2. **A probe**: a way to reveal what the system can or cannot yet hold.
3. **A local target**: a useful endpoint inside a larger condition aim.

The mistake is not having objectives. The mistake is allowing the objective to replace the developmental question.

A practical conversion:

```text
Original objective:
[Ship the onboarding process by September.]

Developmental translation:
[Use the onboarding process to cultivate a condition in which new members can enter the organization, locate boundaries, find support, and begin contributing without a single person carrying all orientation work.]

Local target:
[Ship a usable onboarding process by September.]

Condition aim:
[Orientation becomes distributed, recoverable, and less dependent on hidden expert support.]

Naturalization sign:
[New members can orient each other using shared media and role basins, while edge cases still surface for stewardship rather than disappearing into confusion.]
```

The objective remains. It is no longer the whole aim.

# 4. Attunement: Seeing the Field Before Acting

Attunement asks what you are actually inside.

It protects against beginning from a blank slate when the field already has history, accumulated support, fatigue, expectation, collapse, trust, rhythm, and hidden debt.

Do not begin with:

```text
What should we do?
```

Begin with:

```text
What is already moving?
```

## Core Questions

```text
What am I treating as a single event that may actually be part of a trajectory?

What loop keeps repeating?

What has accumulated from previous loops?

What scale am I observing from?

What can I actually perturb, withdraw, compare, or integrate?

Where might I be mistaking a snapshot for a maintained identity?

What changed because of the last intervention?

What does the system seem able to hold without me?

What depends on my presence, role, prompt, pressure, tool, or support?
```

## What to Notice

Attunement notices conditions, not just events.

Look for:

- repeated patterns;
- support dependence;
- hidden scaffolding;
- changes in friction;
- boundary rigidity or leakage;
- rhythms of collapse and recovery;
- places where the observer is supplying coherence;
- signs that a local action is changing the next beginning.

## The Attunement Mini-Ledger

```text
ATTUNEMENT

Field:

Current concern:

What is visibly happening?

What history is active?

What has changed since the last cycle?

What scale am I observing from?

What can I perturb?

What can I withdraw?

What can I compare?

What can I integrate?

What might I be over-seeing or under-seeing?
```

## Practical Rule

If the same problem keeps returning, do not only solve it again.

Ask:

```text
What condition is making this return necessary?
```

That question often opens the whole practice.

# 5. Aims: Replacing Target States with Condition Aims

Aims give direction without freezing the future.

In Reflexive Coherence Development, the aim is not primarily a final configuration. It is a condition of possibility.

You still arrive. But arrival means entering a regime, not matching a snapshot.

```text
A target is a state to be reached.
An aim is a direction of cultivation.
An orientation is the current vector of attention and action.
Arrival is entry into a condition envelope.
A regime is the stabilized field in which the desired property can arise, vary, recover, and be revised.
```

## Core Questions

```text
What desired emergent property are we orienting toward?

Is this property currently absent, fragile, scaffolded, partial, or native?

What would it mean for this property to arise without being forced?

What would it mean for it to vary without disappearing?

What would it mean for it to recover after perturbation?

What would it mean for it to become more native?

What would arrival look like as a condition, not a final state?
```

## Aim Template

```text
AIM

Desired emergent property:

Current expression:
[Absent / weak / scaffolded / recurring / partially native / native in limited regime]

Condition aim:
What conditions would allow this property to arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer,
or be released appropriately?

Arrival condition:
What would show that the field has entered such a condition regime?

Current external supports:

Missing boundaries/supports/rhythms/media:

Redescription trigger:
What result would tell us the aim needs to change shape?
```

## Hope Statement

Hope becomes practical when it is tied to conditions.

Do not write:

```text
I hope things get better.
```

Write:

```text
I hope that [desired emergent property] can become more native if [condition hypothesis]
is cultivated and tested through [next move / withdrawal / variation].
```

Example:

```text
I hope that coordination can become more native if the team develops a shared boundary map,
weekly integration rhythm, and visible support ledger, then tests whether coordination survives
when manager intervention is reduced.
```

Hope is not magical optimism. It is disciplined orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergence.

## Practical Rule

If the aim can only be described as a desired snapshot, keep asking:

```text
What condition would make this snapshot possible, recoverable, variable, and less forced?
```

# 6. Organization: Turning Aims into Practice Conditions

Organization arranges the field so the aim has somewhere to become real.

When aims are target states, organization tends to become task execution:

```text
objective -> plan -> tasks -> assignments -> metrics -> completion
```

When aims are condition-oriented, organization becomes arrangement of:

- attention;
- supports;
- boundaries;
- roles;
- rhythms;
- shared media;
- reserves;
- probes;
- withdrawal paths;
- integration surfaces.

The unit of practice changes.

| Old planning unit | Reflexive Coherence Development unit |
| --- | --- |
| Task | Cultivation move |
| Role | Support or boundary function |
| Process | Rhythm |
| Resource | Reserve or support ecology |
| Communication | Shared medium |
| Milestone | Condition shift |
| Completion | Naturalization, redescription, or renewed attunement |

## Core Questions

```text
What support is missing?

What support is present but hidden?

What boundary needs to be clarified, softened, strengthened, or made permeable?

What rhythm would allow integration instead of repeated reaction?

What role is carrying too much invisible coherence?

What shared medium carries coordination: documents, traces, timing, signals, norms, tools, space?

What is currently producer-state or scaffold-heavy?

What can be organized so the desired property has somewhere to become native?
```

## Cultivation Moves

A cultivation move is an action that changes a condition or tests whether a condition is real.

Examples:

```text
Disclose a boundary.
Name a support dependency.
Create an integration rhythm.
Weaken a scaffold carefully.
Protect a reserve.
Repair a shared medium.
Clarify a role as a support function.
Test transfer across context.
Reduce naturalization debt.
Redescribe an aim.
```

A cultivation move may include ordinary tasks. The difference is that the task is not the final meaning of the work. It serves condition formation.

## Organization Mini-Ledger

```text
ORGANIZATION

Condition aim:

Missing support:

Hidden support:

Boundary condition:
[Too rigid / too porous / unclear / selectively permeable]

Roles as support functions:

Shared medium:

Rhythm/cadence:

Next cultivation move:

Withdrawal path:

Naturalization debt:
```

## Practical Rule

If the work is busy but the desired property is not becoming more native, ask:

```text
What are we executing that is not forming conditions?
```

# 7. Measures: Evidence Without Mistaking Signs for Capacity

Measurement is necessary. It is also dangerous.

A sign can show that something appeared. It does not by itself show that the relevant capacity has become native.

The measurement shift is:

```text
from endpoint scoring
to claim-ceiling discipline
```

A metric can be useful when it is treated as a sign. It becomes dangerous when it replaces the function it was meant to indicate.

## The Measurement Ladder

```text
Sign
A visible indicator appeared.

Function
The sign is supposed to express some function.

Capacity
The function can recur under some variation.

Native capacity
The system can generate the relevant preconditions with reduced external support.

Regime
The conditions exist such that the capacity can arise, vary, recover, transfer,
and be stewarded without constant forcing.
```

Do not collapse these levels.

## Core Questions

```text
What sign appeared?

What function is the sign supposed to indicate?

What support was present when it appeared?

What happens when conditions vary?

What happens when support is weakened?

Does it transfer to neighboring contexts?

What comparator or control helps separate specific capacity from generic effect?

What can we honestly claim?

What stronger claim remains blocked?
```

## Evidence Template

```text
MEASURES

Desired property:

Observed sign:

Function the sign is supposed to express:

External supports present:

Variation result:

Withdrawal result:

Transfer result:

Comparator/control:

Supported claim:

Blocked stronger claim:

Naturalization status:
[Sign only / scaffolded expression / recurring under support / partial withdrawal survival /
 endogenous support emerging / native in limited regime / broader native regime]

Metric capture risk:
```

## Claim Ceiling Examples

Weak claim:

```text
The property appeared once under strong support.
```

Stronger but still limited claim:

```text
The property recurred across three variations while the original support remained present.
```

Naturalization-oriented claim:

```text
The property survived partial support withdrawal and began generating one of its own preconditions.
```

Blocked stronger claim:

```text
We cannot yet claim native capacity because the property collapses when the scaffold is removed.
```

## Practical Rule

If a metric improves, do not ask only:

```text
Did we succeed?
```

Ask:

```text
What did the metric become evidence of, and what did it not prove?
```

# 8. Stewardship: Caring for Conditions Without Freezing Them

Stewardship begins when something worth caring for has formed or begun to form.

It is not control. It is not preservation of the current form. It is not endless maintenance. It is care for the conditions under which coherent becoming can continue.

Stewardship asks:

```text
What condition now exists that did not exist before?
What does it need in order not to collapse, calcify, or become extractive?
```

## Core Questions

```text
What condition now needs care?

What support should continue?

What support should be weakened?

What support has become dependency?

What boundary is too rigid, too porous, too reactive, or too hidden?

What shared medium is healthy, fatigued, noisy, or degraded?

What agency is being cultivated?

Does that agency strengthen or weaken the surrounding field?

What aim needs redescription?

What should be released rather than maintained?
```

## Agency Questions

Agency is not merely choice. In this practice, agency is treated as persistence, regeneration, transfer, and participation under perturbation.

Ask:

```text
Does this pattern persist only while external support is strong?

Can it recover after disruption?

Can it transfer across context?

Does it generate any of its own supports?

Does its persistence increase or decrease the agency-capacity of the surrounding field?

Is this agency scaffolded, native, proxy, wounded, extractive, or generative?
```

A form of agency is not automatically good because it persists. Some agency persists by weakening the field around it. Stewardship must ask what agency costs and what it makes possible.

## Stewardship Mini-Ledger

```text
STEWARDSHIP

Regime/condition being stewarded:

Native capacities present:

Active dependencies:

Boundary condition:

Support condition:

Shared medium condition:

Agency being cultivated:

Extraction risk:

Rigidity risk:

Collapse risk:

What to support:

What to weaken:

What to repair:

What to release:

Redescription trigger:
```

## Practical Rule

If the main effort becomes preserving the current form, ask:

```text
Are we caring for becoming, or defending a previous arrival?
```

# 9. Renewed Attunement: Returning Without Resetting

Stewardship changes the field.

This is why the spiral returns.

Renewed attunement is not restarting.

| Restarting | Renewed attunement |
| --- | --- |
| Erases history | Reads history |
| Begins again from zero | Begins from changed conditions |
| Repeats the method | Recalibrates the field |
| Treats care as completion | Treats care as field alteration |
| Looks for the same problem again | Looks for what the last cycle made different |

## Core Questions

```text
How did stewardship change the field?

What is now easier to notice?

What is now harder to sustain?

What support debt remains?

What claim ceilings shifted?

What rhythm has become natural?

What artifact or process is becoming bureaucratic?

What scale now needs attention?

What requires renewed attunement before the next aim is set?
```

## Re-Attunement Mini-Ledger

```text
RENEWED ATTUNEMENT

Previous cycle's condition aim:

What changed through organization, measurement, and stewardship?

What became more native?

What remained scaffolded?

What became fragile?

What became rigid?

What changed in observer stance?

What scale now matters?

Next beginning:
```

## Practical Rule

Do not ask:

```text
Are we done?
```

Ask:

```text
What has changed enough that we must now see again?
```

## Ledger Discipline

Keep it short. Fill only what is alive.

Use fragments if needed. Use exact observations when possible. Do not polish the ledger into a story.

The ledger should preserve:

- what was seen;
- what was supplied;
- what changed;
- what survived withdrawal;
- what remained blocked;
- what must be re-attuned.

If maintaining the ledger becomes the work, reduce it.

# 11. Cadence: How Often to Cycle

There is no universal cadence. Cadence must fit the field.

Use the smallest rhythm that preserves contact without creating overhead.

## Four Useful Cadences

### 1. Micro-Cycle

Use during a live session, meeting, experiment, or intervention.

```text
Duration: minutes to hours
Question: What is happening, and what next move keeps contact?
Artifact: a few notes or spoken reflection
```

### 2. Practice Cycle

Use for a recurring team, habit, learning process, clinical phase, agent experiment, or research sprint.

```text
Duration: days to weeks
Question: What condition is forming or failing to form?
Artifact: one ledger entry
```

### 3. Synthesis Cycle

Use after several practice cycles.

```text
Duration: monthly, quarterly, or after a meaningful threshold
Question: What trajectory morphology is visible?
Artifact: short synthesis of accumulation, dependency, and redescription
```

### 4. Regime Cycle

Use when a practice, organization, ecology, or agentic system has formed a stable condition regime.

```text
Duration: seasonal, annual, or event-triggered
Question: What is the regime becoming, and what does it now require of us?
Artifact: stewardship review and renewed attunement
```

## Transition Triggers

Move to renewed attunement when:

- support is withdrawn and the field responds differently;
- a measure changes the claim ceiling;
- a desired property becomes more native;
- an aim becomes impossible or needs redescription;
- a shared medium degrades;
- a role becomes hidden support;
- a rhythm becomes rigid or exhausting;
- a regime stabilizes, collapses, or bifurcates.

## Practical Rule

If cadence creates fatigue, slow down or lighten the artifact.

If cadence disappears, the practice may dissolve into isolated loops.

The right cadence preserves continuity without making continuity bureaucratic.

# 12. Troubleshooting and Failure Modes

Use this section when the practice feels wrong.

| Symptom | Likely issue | Corrective move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We keep doing loops but nothing accumulates | Missing trajectory reading | Re-attune to history, not only the current task |
| The goal feels rigid | Target-state capture | Rewrite it as a condition aim |
| The goal feels vague | Aim without arrival condition | Name what would become possible, recoverable, or native |
| Hope feels like fantasy | Missing condition hypothesis | Name the supports/boundaries needed for desired emergence |
| Hope collapses under evidence | Snapshot hope | Redescribe hope as condition formation |
| Metrics improve but capacity feels fragile | Sign/capacity confusion | Add withdrawal, variation, and transfer tests |
| Everyone is busy but the field is unchanged | Task capture | Ask what conditions the tasks are forming |
| The process feels bureaucratic | Artifact capture | Reduce the ledger to contact questions |
| Everything works only with one person present | Hidden support | Name the role's support function and design withdrawal/substitution |
| Stability is being protected too strongly | Stewardship rigidity | Re-attune to what the regime may be preventing |
| The spiral feels exhausting | Spiral fatigue | Lengthen cadence, reduce explicitness, check reserve and medium health |
| The same solution is applied at every scale | Scale mismatch | Recalibrate observer stance and claim ceiling |
| The practice feels familiar but not alive | Habitat illusion | Test withdrawal, novelty response, and medium health |
| The system resists re-attunement | Return avoidance | Ask what stewardship is protecting from being seen |

## Five Common Collapses

### 1. Sign Becomes Capacity

```text
It appeared, therefore it is real capacity.
```

Correction:

```text
What support was present? What survives variation and withdrawal?
```

### 2. Target Becomes Aim

```text
The deliverable is the development.
```

Correction:

```text
What condition should the deliverable help form?
```

### 3. Ledger Becomes Contact

```text
We completed the artifact, therefore we practiced.
```

Correction:

```text
What did the artifact preserve contact with?
```

### 4. Stewardship Becomes Preservation

```text
The regime exists, so protect its current form.
```

Correction:

```text
What becoming does this form now enable or prevent?
```

### 5. Return Becomes Reset

```text
Start over.
```

Correction:

```text
Begin again from the changed field.
```

# 13. Domain Examples

These are not full case studies. They show how the same structure translates across domains.

## 13.1 AI / Agentic Systems

```text
Field:
An agentic system solves planning tasks well under a structured prompt and tool scaffold.

Desired emergent property:
Reliable decomposition and recovery across task variants.

Condition aim:
The system generates relevant structure without the prompt supplying all decomposition.

Organization:
Vary prompts, reduce explicit decomposition, introduce transfer tasks, track naturalization debt in tool/state scaffolds.

Measures:
Compare full scaffold, weakened scaffold, altered context, and no-scaffold performance. Track failure modes and claim ceilings.

Stewardship:
Keep useful supports while identifying which ones should become geometry, memory-like aftereffect, role basin, or endogenous strategy.

Renewed attunement:
After scaffold weakening, ask what the system now reveals about native structure-generation rather than simply whether it passed.
```

## 13.2 Team / Organization

```text
Field:
A team coordinates only when the manager resolves ambiguity.

Desired emergent property:
Local coordination under changing conditions.

Condition aim:
Roles, boundaries, shared media, and rhythms allow coordination to regenerate without constant managerial intervention.

Organization:
Create visible decision boundaries, shared issue traces, weekly integration rhythm, and role-based support functions.

Measures:
Observe what happens when manager intervention is reduced. Track latency, unresolved ambiguity, peer-to-peer resolution, and hidden support.

Stewardship:
Prevent the new rhythm from becoming ritual. Care for shared medium health and role elasticity.

Renewed attunement:
Ask whether the manager's absence now reveals native coordination, new dependencies, or merely displaced confusion.
```

## 13.3 Personal Creative Practice

```text
Field:
Creative work happens only under urgency or crisis.

Desired emergent property:
Sustainable creative recurrence.

Condition aim:
Rhythms, reserves, boundaries, and recovery conditions allow creation to return without requiring panic.

Organization:
Protect time, lower initiation cost, create integration notes, maintain recovery windows, reduce dependency on deadline pressure.

Measures:
Track recurrence after interruption, under lower pressure, and across different contexts. Do not treat one productive day as native capacity.

Stewardship:
Protect rest and reserve. Notice when discipline becomes rigidity or when output replaces aliveness.

Renewed attunement:
Ask what changed in the relationship between pressure, attention, and creation.
```

## 13.4 Clinical / Regulation Practice

```text
Field:
A person can regulate during supported sessions but collapses after real-world perturbation.

Desired emergent property:
Regulation that can return after perturbation.

Condition aim:
Internal, relational, environmental, and rhythmic supports allow recovery without the practitioner supplying all regulation.

Organization:
Identify supports, practice graded perturbation, create recovery cues, involve safe relational/environmental conditions where appropriate.

Measures:
Track latency to recovery, support dependence, transfer outside session, and collapse thresholds.

Stewardship:
Avoid mistaking session calm for native regulation. Support carefully; withdraw gradually; redescribe aims when the field shows what is not yet possible.

Renewed attunement:
Ask what the person now generates themselves and where support remains essential.
```

This guide is not clinical advice. Domain expertise, consent, safety, and professional judgment remain necessary.

## 13.5 Ecological Restoration

```text
Field:
A restoration site improves while intervention remains intensive.

Desired emergent property:
Self-sustaining regenerative dynamics.

Condition aim:
Soil, water, species relations, disturbance rhythms, and boundary conditions allow recovery processes to continue with reduced external input.

Organization:
Protect key supports, reduce damaging pressures, introduce or restore missing relations, monitor seasonal rhythms.

Measures:
Track not only abundance but recurrence, recruitment, resilience after perturbation, dependency on intervention, and cross-species coupling.

Stewardship:
Avoid forcing one desired stable image. Care for trajectory diversity and controlled succession.

Renewed attunement:
Ask what the ecosystem is beginning to generate and what still depends on external supply.
```

## 13.6 Research Program

```text
Field:
A research project accumulates observations but does not produce stable concepts.

Desired emergent property:
Generative class vocabulary.

Condition aim:
Observations can be classified, compared, promoted, retired, and integrated into reusable theoretical structure.

Organization:
Maintain observation logs, value-gate classes, schedule synthesis passes, test whether new terms reorganize old cases.

Measures:
Track class promotion, explanatory reuse, blocked stronger claims, and whether concepts survive new cases.

Stewardship:
Prevent premature theory closure and endless local tagging. Care for the boundary between exploratory language and claims.

Renewed attunement:
Ask how the new vocabulary changes what can now be observed.
```


## Role Distribution

Do not turn the spiral into departments. But in a group, different people may hold different sensitivities.

```text
Attunement holder:
Notices field shifts, history, scale, hidden assumptions.

Aim holder:
Keeps direction tied to condition formation rather than output fixation.

Organization holder:
Tracks supports, boundaries, rhythms, roles, and next cultivation moves.

Measure holder:
Protects claim ceilings and prevents signs from becoming inflated claims.

Stewardship holder:
Notices care, reserve, release, extraction, rigidity, and redescription needs.
```

These are temporary functions, not fixed identities.

Rotate them when useful.

---

# 15. What Not to Do

Do not use this guide to make every simple task philosophical.

If the domain is predefined and execution is the right mode, execute.

Do not replace all metrics with vague reflection.

Use metrics. But keep their claim ceilings honest.

Do not treat condition-oriented aims as avoidance of commitment.

Aims should orient action. They should not dissolve into endless openness.

Do not treat emergence as an excuse for lack of responsibility.

When you intervene in a field, you participate in what happens next.

Do not treat native capacity as a moral demand.

Some systems need support. Withdrawal can become abandonment if done without care.

Do not confuse agency with goodness.

Agency can be scaffolded, native, proxy, wounded, extractive, or generative.

Do not make the ledger sacred.

The field is primary. The artifact serves contact.

# 16. Glossary for Practice

## Aim

A direction of cultivation. An aim orients activity without freezing the future into a final snapshot.

## Arrival

Entry into a condition regime. Arrival does not mean final completion; it means the field now supports a desired form of becoming more natively.

## Attunement

The discipline of reading the field before acting: history, scale, support, boundary, trajectory, and observer stance.

## Boundary

A condition of selective separation and relation. Boundaries can be too rigid, too porous, too hidden, or appropriately permeable.

## Claim Ceiling

The highest interpretation the evidence currently supports. Claim ceilings protect against overclaim.

## Condition Aim

A description of the conditions under which a desired emergent property can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, or be released.

## Cultivation Move

An action that changes a condition or tests whether a condition is real.

## Desired Emergent Property

A property that cannot be assumed to appear simply by command or target specification, but may become possible under appropriate conditions.

## Hope

Disciplined orientation toward naturalized conditions for desired emergent properties.

## Native Capacity

A capacity whose relevant preconditions are generated by the system or regime itself, rather than supplied entirely from outside.

## Naturalization Debt

The remaining dependence on explicit scaffold, producer state, rule, tool, role, prompt, or support that has not yet become native condition.

## Regime

A stabilized condition field that supports certain trajectories, capacities, rhythms, boundaries, and agencies.

## Shared Medium

The field through which coordination is carried: traces, documents, signals, timing, norms, reserves, gradients, tools, spaces, or other support surfaces.

## Stewardship

Care of condition regimes without reducing care to control, preservation, maintenance, or extraction.

## Support

Anything that allows a property to appear or persist: scaffold, relationship, tool, rhythm, boundary, role, prompt, environment, reserve, or shared medium.

## Trajectory

The path-dependent accumulation of loops. A trajectory is not a line of snapshots; it is the changing condition of future loops.

## Withdrawal

The disciplined weakening or removal of support to test what is scaffolded, dependent, emerging, or native. Withdrawal must be careful; it is not abandonment.

# 17. Final Practice Formulation

Reflexive Coherence Development can be practiced with one recurring question:

```text
What conditions would allow the desired property to become more native,
and what did our last action change about those conditions?
```

Everything else supports that question.

Attunement keeps the field visible.

Aims keep direction alive without freezing the future.

Organization arranges supports, boundaries, roles, rhythms, and media.

Measures protect evidence from inflation and proxy capture.

Stewardship cares for what forms without freezing it.

Renewed attunement returns to the changed field.

The spiral is not a path to complete. It is a rhythm for staying honest inside development.

```text
Do not only ask what to achieve.
Ask what to make possible.

Do not only ask whether it appeared.
Ask what supported it.

Do not only ask whether it worked.
Ask what became more native.

Do not only ask whether the cycle is complete.
Ask what the cycle changed.
```

The work begins again from there.
