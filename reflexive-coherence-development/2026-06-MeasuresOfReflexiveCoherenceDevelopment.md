## Abstract

*Attunement of Becoming* changed the unit of sight. A cultivation loop is not an isolated episode but a local aperture into a continuous, path-dependent, scale-coupled field. Loops integrate into trajectories; trajectories integrate into regimes; regimes interact as ecologies; and the observer is a scale-calibrated participant whose probes, supports, withdrawals, classifications, and integrations become part of the field they observe.

*Aims of Reflexive Coherence Development* changed the unit of direction. Development remains oriented, but not primarily toward terminal configurations. It is condition-teleological: it moves toward regimes of possibility in which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native.

*Organization of Reflexive Coherence Development* changed the unit of practice. If the aim is a condition regime, organization cannot be reduced only to task execution toward an endpoint. It must arrange activity, support, boundary, rhythm, role, shared medium, withdrawal, and integration around condition formation.

This paper asks what happens to **measurement** after those three shifts.

If development is understood as movement toward a predefined result, measurement can often be understood as endpoint scoring: define the target, choose a metric, track distance, verify completion, and compare performance. This remains powerful and often appropriate when the object being measured is stable, the metric is validated against the function, the support conditions are known, and the result itself is the correct unit of evaluation. Reflexive Coherence Development does not reject metrics. It names their domain.

The gap appears when the visible sign and the developmental property diverge. A score can improve while the capacity remains scaffolded. A behavior can recur while the support ecology carries the function. A team can complete tasks while degrading the shared medium. An agent can produce goal-like output while producer state supplies the missing agency. A person can appear regulated because the context supplies all boundary integrity. A regime can look stable because bifurcation has been suppressed rather than integrated.

This paper develops **Measures of Reflexive Coherence Development** as the discipline of measuring signs without mistaking them for functions, measuring functions without overclaiming capacity, measuring capacity without ignoring support, and measuring regimes without freezing them into scalar targets.

The central claim is:

```text
Measurement in Reflexive Coherence Development is not endpoint scoring.
It is claim-ceiling discipline: the practice of asking what a sign is evidence of,
under what supports, across what variation, with what withdrawal behavior,
and with which stronger claims still blocked.
```

From this shift, several consequences follow. Evidence must preserve its support context. Metrics become instruments rather than authorities. Claim ceilings become more important than scores. Support, comparator behavior, withdrawal response, transfer, recurrence, and trajectory morphology become part of what is measured. Agency is measured as persistence, regeneration, transfer, support-generation, and generative participation under perturbation, not merely as choice-like output. Hope becomes measurable only through condition hypotheses and naturalization evidence, not through optimism. Organization is measured by condition formation and naturalization debt, not merely by task completion.

This paper forms the fourth ground of Reflexive Coherence Development. Attunement changes sight. Aims change direction. Organization changes practice. Measures change evidence. Once evidence is no longer reduced to endpoint scores, the next question becomes how to care for the regimes that evidence discloses. That is the work of Stewardship.

## 1. Why Measurement Must Change After Attunement, Aims, and Organization

Measurement is where a development practice tells itself what is real.

For that reason, measurement is never a neutral afterthought. It decides what becomes visible, what becomes ignorable, what can be claimed, what is treated as progress, what is treated as noise, what receives support, what loses support, and what becomes optimized. A measure does not merely record a field. In developmental systems, it often enters the field as pressure, attention, incentive, comparison, rhythm, and expectation.

This is not a problem in itself. It is unavoidable. The question is whether measurement is honest about what it is doing.

Attunement showed that a cultivation loop is not an isolated episode. Each observation, probe, withdrawal, classification, and integration changes the field that later loops inherit. If this is true, then a measurement cannot be understood only as a reading taken from outside. The measurement is also an event inside the trajectory. It may alter trust, attention, support dependence, class vocabulary, role behavior, and future orientation.

Aims showed that development is not primarily movement toward a terminal configuration. It is movement toward a condition regime in which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native. If this is true, then a measurement cannot ask only whether a target state has appeared. It must ask whether the conditions that would make the desired property native are forming.

Organization showed that activity is not only task execution. It is the arrangement of support, boundary, rhythm, role, shared medium, withdrawal, and integration around condition formation. If this is true, then measurement cannot look only at deliverables. It must ask what activity is doing to the conditions under which future activity occurs.

Measurement must change because the object has changed.

A predefined-world measure asks:

```text
Did the system reach the specified result?
How much of the task is complete?
How far is the current state from the target state?
How does this output compare to another output?
```

These questions are valid when the state space is known, the target is stable, the output class is predefined, the metric is well aligned with the function, and support conditions are not the main object of development. In such domains, endpoint metrics can be clear, efficient, and responsible. They protect against vagueness. They make coordination possible. They allow comparison, improvement, and accountability.

Reflexive Coherence Development begins where those conditions are not enough.

The developmental question is not only:

```text
Was the output produced?
```

It is:

```text
What is the output evidence of?
```

That second question opens the measurement problem.

An output may be evidence that a task was performed. It may be evidence that a scaffold was effective. It may be evidence that a hidden support carried the function. It may be evidence that a proxy was optimized. It may be evidence that the system can produce a sign under supplied conditions. It may be evidence that a capacity is beginning to naturalize. It may be evidence that a regime has formed. It may be evidence that a neighboring regime has been depleted.

The same visible result can support different claims depending on the supports, probes, contexts, comparators, withdrawal behavior, and temporal history surrounding it.

That is why measurement in Reflexive Coherence Development is not primarily about finding the right number. It is about preserving the relation between sign, function, support, capacity, regime, and claim.

A sign can be true and still overclaimed.

A metric can be accurate and still insufficient.

A score can improve and still indicate developmental regression if the improvement is purchased by increasing hidden support, narrowing context, suppressing variation, degrading the shared medium, or shifting agency from the system into the measurement apparatus.

A measurement becomes unsafe when it is asked to carry a claim its evidence cannot support.

This is the essential proposition of this paper.

```text
Measurement must change because development is not only visible change.
It is condition change, and condition change cannot be inferred from signs alone.
```

The observer must therefore measure with claim ceilings.

A claim ceiling is the strongest interpretation justified by the evidence as gathered. It says not only what was observed, but what the observation is allowed to mean. It keeps the measure from climbing too quickly from event to capacity, from recurrence to naturalization, from output to agency, from stability to health, from correlation to coupling, from compliance to practice, or from agreement to shared medium.

Without claim ceilings, measurement pulls development back into snapshot logic.

A metric sees a state. A claim ceiling asks what that state can honestly claim.

A metric sees an output. A claim ceiling asks what support produced it.

A metric sees recurrence. A claim ceiling asks whether recurrence survived variation, withdrawal, and transfer.

A metric sees success. A claim ceiling asks whether the conditions of future success became more native or more dependent.

This changes the emotional tone of measurement as well. Measurement is no longer the final judge that declares success or failure. It becomes a disciplined way of staying in contact with what is forming, what is scaffolded, what is blocked, what is decaying, what is native, what is overclaimed, and what must be redescribed.

The purpose of measurement is not to replace attunement. It is to protect attunement from wishful interpretation.

The purpose of measurement is not to replace aims. It is to keep aims honest about the conditions they require.

The purpose of measurement is not to replace organization. It is to reveal whether organization is producing outputs, forming conditions, hiding supports, or accumulating naturalization debt.

The purpose of measurement is not to replace stewardship. It is to disclose what stewardship must care for.

If Attunement changes sight, Aims change direction, and Organization changes practice, then Measures change evidence.

They teach the development practice to ask:

```text
What does this sign show?
What does it not show?
What support made it possible?
What survives when support changes?
What transfers across context?
What becomes easier without being forced?
What stronger claim remains blocked?
```

Only after those questions are preserved can measurement serve Reflexive Coherence Development rather than recapturing it into endpoint scoring.

## 2. The Domain and Boundary of Endpoint Metrics

Endpoint metrics belong to a real and important domain.

They are strongest when the object of development is already well formed. The target is known. The relevant variables are stable. The success criterion is accepted. The measurement surface has already been validated. The role of support is either controlled, irrelevant to the claim, or explicitly included in the specification. The observer can compare outputs without fundamentally changing what the output means.

In that domain, the structure is straightforward:

```text
current state -> measured distance -> target state
```

A metric can be represented as a map:

$$
\mu : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}
$$

where $\mathcal{X}$ is the space of relevant outputs or states and $\mu(x)$ assigns a value to a state $x$. If the target is $x^*$, development can be locally organized around reducing distance, improving score, satisfying threshold, or reaching compliance:

$$
\mu(x_t) \to \mu(x^*)
$$

or, when the metric is a distance,

$$
d(x_t, x^*) \to 0.
$$

This is not a deficient form. It is an appropriate form when $x^*$ is the right object, $d$ measures what matters, and reaching $x^*$ is sufficient for the claim being made.

Examples are common.

A manufactured part must remain within tolerance. A file must conform to a format. A transaction must settle. A bridge must meet structural requirements. A unit test must pass. A safety procedure must restore a known safe condition. A package must be delivered. A known defect must be repaired. A constrained model must meet a benchmark that has been accepted as the local evaluation surface.

In such cases, endpoint metrics are not only useful. They are often ethically necessary. They make commitments inspectable. They prevent ambiguity from hiding poor execution. They let people coordinate across distance, expertise, and time. They support responsibility.

Reflexive Coherence Development does not weaken this domain. It preserves it by refusing to make endpoint metrics carry claims outside it.

The boundary appears when the measured state is not the developmental object.

A metric $\mu(x)$ observes a projection of the field. It does not by itself reveal the condition that produced $x$, the support that sustained it, the boundary that made it possible, the hidden dependency it required, the neighboring regime it drew from, or the future capacity it formed.

A more complete developmental observation has the form:

$$
o_t = \Omega(M_t, q_t, s_t, c_t)
$$

where:

- $M_t$ is the developmental condition of the system-observer field at time $t$,
- $q_t$ is the question, probe, task, or perturbation applied,
- $s_t$ is the support or scaffold present,
- $c_t$ is the context, including rhythm, medium, and surrounding regime,
- $o_t$ is the observed expression.

A metric then reads only some aspect of the observation:

$$
\mu(o_t).
$$

The metric may be precise. But precision over $o_t$ does not automatically recover $M_t$. The developmental condition is not visible from the score alone.

This is the projection problem.

A projection is not false. It is partial. The danger appears when the projection is treated as the whole object.

In predefined domains, that partiality may be acceptable because the unmeasured conditions are stable, controlled, or irrelevant to the claim. In open-ended developmental domains, those unmeasured conditions are often the object itself.

A calm face may project regulation. It may also project suppression, freeze, dissociation, social compliance, or dependence on another person's boundary.

A high benchmark score may project competence. It may also project prompt fit, dataset familiarity, tool dependence, scaffolded reasoning, or benchmark-specific proxy optimization.

A completed task list may project organizational progress. It may also project task capture, hidden coordination burden, role exhaustion, medium degradation, or deferred integration.

A stable community process may project governance maturity. It may also project conflict avoidance, suppressed bifurcation, overcentralized mediation, or dependence on a single person.

An agent's autonomous behavior may project agency. It may also project producer-state scaffolding, hidden rule tables, role scripts, external memory, or environment-shaped affordances that do not belong to the agentic basin being claimed.

Endpoint metrics become incomplete when they are used to evaluate properties whose truth depends on support, variation, withdrawal, transfer, recurrence, context, or regime formation.

The issue is not measurement versus no measurement.

The issue is whether the measurement preserves the developmental object.

A metric is locally sufficient when:

```text
the target class is known,
the measured sign reliably tracks the relevant function,
the support conditions are stable or included in the claim,
the observer's intervention does not supply the property being measured,
the property does not need to be distinguished as scaffolded or native,
and the score is not being treated as evidence for a stronger claim.
```

A metric becomes developmentally insufficient when:

```text
the target class is still emerging,
the sign and function may diverge,
the support conditions may carry the expression,
the observer's probe may supply the missing boundary,
the property must become native,
the surrounding field may be strengthened or depleted,
or the score is used to claim agency, competence, maturity, resilience, or regime health.
```

The task of Measures is therefore not to replace metrics with vaguer language. It is to add the missing dimensions that metrics alone cannot carry.

Measure the output, but record the support.

Measure the recurrence, but test variation.

Measure the performance, but weaken the scaffold.

Measure the coordination, but inspect the shared medium.

Measure the agent, but expose producer-state residue.

Measure the stability, but ask whether bifurcation is being suppressed.

Measure the hope, but ask what condition hypothesis has become testable.

Endpoint metrics remain valid inside their domain. Reflexive Coherence Development begins at their boundary and asks what else must be preserved for the claim to remain honest.

## 3. Sign, Function, Capacity, and Regime

The first measurement discipline is to separate sign, function, capacity, native capacity, and regime.

A **sign** is an observable expression. It is what appears: a score, behavior, utterance, output, posture, completion, agreement, movement, recurrence, or visible state.

A **function** is what the sign is taken to express or accomplish. It is the role the sign is supposed to play in the system: regulation, understanding, repair, reasoning, coordination, agency, resilience, learning, trust, cooperation, abundance, governance, or ecological recovery.

A **capacity** is the ability to perform or regenerate the function across relevant variation. It is not only the sign appearing once. It is the function holding under changes in context, timing, support, perturbation, or demand.

A **native capacity** is a capacity whose relevant preconditions are generated by the system or regime itself rather than supplied externally by the observer, scaffold, prompt, rule table, protected context, or hidden neighboring regime.

A **regime** is the condition-field that sustains native capacity across time, trajectories, boundaries, rhythms, and interactions.

These are not interchangeable.

The measurement ladder is:

```text
sign -> function -> capacity -> native capacity -> regime
```

Each rung requires evidence that the previous rung does not supply by itself.

A sign does not prove function.

Function under support does not prove capacity.

Capacity under narrow variation does not prove native capacity.

Native capacity in one path does not prove regime health.

Regime stability does not prove ecological coherence.

This ladder is the antidote to measurement inflation.

Consider regulation. A person appears calm. The calm expression is a sign. It may express regulation, but it may also express suppression, exhaustion, compliance, dissociation, or environmental protection. To claim regulation, the observer must ask how the system responds to perturbation, whether recovery occurs, what support is present, and whether calm can return after disturbance. To claim native regulation, the observer must ask whether the person can generate the relevant boundary and recovery conditions without the clinician, room, script, or relationship carrying them. To claim a regulation regime, the observer must ask whether rhythms, relationships, practices, and environmental conditions sustain recovery across contexts.

Consider learning. A student solves a problem. The solution is a sign. It may express understanding, but it may also express pattern recall, cue-following, or local coaching. To claim capacity, the observer must test variation and transfer. To claim native capacity, the student must generate the representation, error detection, and repair moves without the instructor supplying them. To claim a learning regime, the surrounding conditions must let inquiry, correction, consolidation, and transfer recur.

Consider AI behavior. A model produces a correct answer. The answer is a sign. It may express reasoning, but it may also express memorization, prompt scaffolding, tool supply, benchmark familiarity, or role-script completion. To claim capacity, the behavior must survive variation and perturbation. To claim native agency, the system must generate relevant orientation, boundary, repair, and support structures rather than relying on producer state. To claim an agentic ecology, coordination must be sustained by shared medium, role basins, affordances, reserves, and aftereffects rather than only explicit orchestration.

Consider organization. A team completes a project. Completion is a sign. It may express coordination, but it may also express heroics, hidden support, role overloading, suppressed disagreement, or medium depletion. To claim organizational capacity, similar work must be possible across variation without the same hidden carriers. To claim native practice, the organization must regenerate the relevant supports, boundaries, roles, rhythms, and repairs. To claim a regime, multiple trajectories must be sustainable without homogenization or extraction.

Measurement must therefore ask at every point:

```text
Which rung is this evidence allowed to support?
```

The temptation is always to climb.

A sign feels meaningful, so it is called a function.

A function appears under support, so it is called capacity.

A capacity recurs in a familiar context, so it is called native.

A native path stabilizes, so it is called a regime.

A regime persists, so it is called healthy.

Measures of Reflexive Coherence Development interrupts that climb.

It does not deny the higher possibility. It blocks premature claim.

The sign may become evidence of function, but only if function is tested.

The function may become evidence of capacity, but only if variation is tested.

The capacity may become evidence of native capacity, but only if support weakening and endogenous precondition generation are tested.

The native capacity may become evidence of regime, but only if recurrence, rhythm, boundary, shared medium, and cross-path sustainability are tested.

A simple table can preserve the distinctions:

| Rung | Question | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign** | What appeared? | Observation, trace, output, state, behavior |
| **Function** | What did the sign do? | Relation to intended function, context, perturbation response |
| **Capacity** | Can the function recur across variation? | Variation, recurrence, transfer, repair behavior |
| **Native capacity** | Can the system generate the preconditions? | Withdrawal, support substitution, endogenous support evidence |
| **Regime** | Are conditions sustained across paths and time? | Rhythm, boundary, shared medium, cross-path diversity, recovery |

The table is not a scoring device. It is a claim discipline.

A low rung is not bad. A sign is not a failure because it is only a sign. It becomes useful precisely when it is allowed to remain what it is. A scaffolded function is not a failure because it is scaffolded. It may be the first evidence that a capacity is conditionally possible. A capacity that is not yet native is not failure. It tells the observer where support still lives.

Measurement becomes harmful when it needs the evidence to be more mature than it is.

In Reflexive Coherence Development, the measurement act must preserve immaturity without contempt. It must preserve possibility without inflation. It must preserve support without shame. It must preserve blocked claims without despair.

The sign is honored by not making it carry the whole future.

## 4. Claim Ceilings as Measurement Discipline

A measurement is not only a value. It is a claim.

The score, count, observation, rating, trace, or comparison becomes meaningful only when interpreted. The interpretation may be modest or inflated. It may stay near the evidence or climb beyond it. Claim ceilings name the highest rung the evidence can support without overreach.

A claim ceiling has this form:

```text
Given this observation, under these supports, with these comparators,
across this variation, after this withdrawal, over this time horizon,
the strongest valid claim is: [claim].
The following stronger claims remain blocked: [blocked claims].
```

This structure matters more than the number itself.

A score of 92% is not self-interpreting. It may support the claim that the system performed well on a task under the given conditions. It may not support the claim that the system understands, can transfer, has native agency, or has formed a regime.

A stable mood rating is not self-interpreting. It may support the claim that distress was low in that context. It may not support the claim that regulation capacity has naturalized.

A high employee engagement score is not self-interpreting. It may support the claim that participants reported positive sentiment under the current survey conditions. It may not support the claim that the shared medium is healthy, disagreement is metabolized, or agency is generative.

A repeated agent output is not self-interpreting. It may support the claim that a behavior is reproducible under the current prompt, tool, role, memory, and environment. It may not support the claim that the agentic basin persists without producer state.

Claim ceilings can be organized as a measurement ladder:

| Ceiling | Claim type | Example wording |
|---|---|---|
| **C0: Observation** | A sign appeared | “This output/behavior/state occurred under specified conditions.” |
| **C1: Recurrence** | The sign repeated | “The sign recurred across similar trials or moments.” |
| **C2: Supported function** | The sign plausibly performed a function under support | “Under this scaffold, the expression served the intended function.” |
| **C3: Specific function** | Comparator behavior supports specificity | “The function appears specific to this boundary/support relation, not generic noise.” |
| **C4: Variable capacity** | Function survived relevant variation | “The function held across changes in context, prompt, role, perturbation, or timing.” |
| **C5: Withdrawal-resistant capacity** | Function survived support weakening | “The capacity partially held as external support was reduced or substituted.” |
| **C6: Native capacity** | System generated relevant preconditions | “The system began producing the boundary/support conditions previously supplied from outside.” |
| **C7: Regime expression** | Capacity is sustained by a condition-field | “The capacity recurs through rhythm, boundary, medium, and cross-path support without constant external forcing.” |
| **C8: Ecological effect** | Regime affects surrounding agency-capacity | “The regime strengthens, weakens, or reshapes neighboring regimes and shared media.” |

The exact labels can be revised by domain. The discipline is stable: do not let evidence at a lower ceiling speak as though it has reached a higher one.

Claim ceilings also require blocked stronger claims.

A measurement record is incomplete if it says only what is supported. It must also say what is not yet supported.

For example:

```text
SUPPORTED CLAIM:
The agent produced a correct plan under the supplied role prompt and tool access.

BLOCKED CLAIMS:
This does not yet show native planning capacity.
This does not yet show role-basin persistence.
This does not yet show transfer without the prompt.
This does not yet show generative agency.
```

Or:

```text
SUPPORTED CLAIM:
The team completed the release while maintaining schedule and quality thresholds.

BLOCKED CLAIMS:
This does not yet show that delivery capacity is native.
This does not yet show that coordination burden was distributed.
This does not yet show that the shared medium was strengthened.
This does not yet show that the practice can recur without hidden heroics.
```

Blocked claims are not negative decorations. They are part of the evidence.

They prevent premature arrival.

They preserve the path ahead.

They keep hope actionable rather than magical.

They allow organization to ask what support must still be cultivated.

They give stewardship a map of what not to preserve falsely.

A claim ceiling can be written formally as a constraint on interpretation. Let $E$ be the evidence gathered, and let $\mathcal{K}$ be the ordered set of possible claims from observation to ecological effect. A claim-ceiling function assigns the highest warranted claim:

$$
\operatorname{ceil}(E) = \max\{k \in \mathcal{K} : E \text{ warrants claim } k\}.
$$

This notation does not imply that warrant is mechanically decidable. It names the discipline: interpretation must not exceed evidence.

A metric value $\mu(o)$ is therefore not the claim. It is one part of $E$.

The evidence bundle must include:

```text
observation,
metric value,
probe or task,
support/scaffold present,
context and rhythm,
comparators or controls,
variation tested,
withdrawal behavior,
recurrence history,
transfer behavior,
observer stance,
and blocked stronger claims.
```

Only then can the claim ceiling be assigned responsibly.

Claim ceilings also protect against the misuse of formal language. Equations, ledgers, and metrics can make a weak claim appear stronger than it is. A precise number with missing support context is not a strong measurement. It is a precise sign with an unknown ceiling.

The strongest measurement is not the one with the most numbers.

The strongest measurement is the one whose interpretation stays closest to what the evidence can actually bear.

## 5. Scaffolded Evidence and Native Evidence

Support is part of the evidence.

This is the second major measurement discipline. No developmental observation should be recorded as if it occurred in an empty field. A property appears under conditions. Those conditions may include explicit scaffolds, implicit expectations, social safety, prompts, tools, rules, memory, role tables, trained routines, institutional protection, environmental affordances, time pressure, incentives, rhythms, and neighboring regimes.

If the support is not recorded, the claim will usually climb too high.

A supported expression can be valuable. It may reveal possibility. It may show that the system can enter a region of the basin when supplied with a missing boundary. It may disclose what kind of support is needed. It may expose a path toward naturalization.

But supported possibility is not native capacity.

This distinction can be expressed through a support-response curve. Let $p$ be a desired property or function. Let $\sigma$ represent the level or kind of external support, where higher $\sigma$ means more external scaffold and lower $\sigma$ means weakened, substituted, or withdrawn scaffold. Let $\rho_p(\sigma, c)$ represent the degree to which property $p$ is expressed in context $c$ under support $\sigma$.

$$
\rho_p : \Sigma \times \mathcal{C} \to [0,1]
$$

This is not necessarily a probability. It can be a qualitative or ordinal reading of expression strength, coherence, recurrence, or functional adequacy.

A scaffolded property may show:

$$
\rho_p(\sigma_{high}, c) \approx 1
$$

but

$$
\rho_p(\sigma_{low}, c) \approx 0.
$$

The sign appears under high support and collapses when support is weakened.

A capacity beginning to naturalize shows a different pattern. Expression may remain partial, but it does not collapse immediately as support is weakened. The system may generate substitutes, repair routes, or endogenous preconditions:

$$
\rho_p(\sigma_{high}, c) > \rho_p(\sigma_{mid}, c) > \rho_p(\sigma_{low}, c)
$$

with meaningful persistence at lower support and evidence of endogenous support formation.

Native capacity is not the absence of support. Nothing develops without conditions. Native capacity means that the relevant support has shifted from being externally supplied to being generated, maintained, or recruited by the system or regime itself.

The measurement question is therefore not:

```text
Can the property appear without any support?
```

It is:

```text
Where does the support live?
```

External support is not bad. It is often necessary. But it must be named.

Internalized or endogenous support is not magic. It is condition formation.

A support can move through several statuses:

| Status | Description | Measurement question |
|---|---|---|
| **Unseen support** | Support exists but is not recorded | What is carrying the expression? |
| **External scaffold** | Observer/context supplies missing condition | What appears only because this support is present? |
| **Weakened scaffold** | Support is reduced or varied | What survives partial withdrawal? |
| **Substituted support** | Different support performs similar function | Is the capacity tied to one scaffold or the function it supplied? |
| **Endogenous support** | System begins generating the precondition | What boundary/support is now produced internally? |
| **Regime support** | Condition field sustains the capacity | Does the support recur through rhythm, medium, relation, and practice? |
| **Ecological support** | Neighboring regimes participate without extraction | Does the capacity strengthen or weaken surrounding conditions? |

This table prevents two opposite errors.

The first error is **support erasure**. The property is treated as native because the scaffold is invisible.

The second error is **support contempt**. The property is dismissed because it is scaffolded.

Both distort development.

Support erasure inflates claims. Support contempt destroys pathways.

A scaffolded expression should be recorded as scaffolded expression. That may be exactly the evidence needed at this stage. The observer now knows that the property is conditionally possible. The next question becomes whether the support can be weakened, substituted, internalized, or transformed into regime condition.

Measurement should therefore include explicit support tests:

1. **Variation test**: Does the function survive changes in context, prompt, role, medium, timing, or perturbation?
2. **Withdrawal test**: What happens when the scaffold is weakened or removed?
3. **Substitution test**: Can a different support perform the same function?
4. **Delay test**: Does the function recur after time has passed and the immediate scaffold is gone?
5. **Transfer test**: Does the capacity appear in a new context where the original cues are absent?
6. **Endogenous-precondition test**: Does the system generate a boundary, support, representation, repair, or rhythm that the observer previously supplied?
7. **Comparator test**: Does the effect appear specifically in the probed system or generically across controls?

These tests do not produce final certainty. They raise or lower claim ceilings.

A property that survives variation but not withdrawal may be a supported capacity.

A property that survives withdrawal but not transfer may be local native capacity.

A property that transfers but degrades surrounding regimes may be extractive agency.

A property that recurs, transfers, repairs, and strengthens surrounding conditions may support a higher claim of generative agency or regime formation.

The evidence must say which.

This is why Measures must preserve the full evidence bundle:

$$
E_t = (o_t, \mu_t, q_t, s_t, c_t, v_t, w_t, \kappa_t, h_t)
$$

where:

- $o_t$ is the observed expression,
- $\mu_t$ is any metric or score applied to it,
- $q_t$ is the probe, task, or perturbation,
- $s_t$ is the support or scaffold condition,
- $c_t$ is the context and surrounding regime,
- $v_t$ is the variation tested,
- $w_t$ is the withdrawal or support-reduction behavior,
- $\kappa_t$ is comparator or control behavior,
- $h_t$ is the relevant history carried from prior loops.

The metric $\mu_t$ is one element. It is not the evidence as a whole.

A development measure that forgets support forgets development.

## 6. Agency Diagnostics

Agency is one of the central properties that Reflexive Coherence Development may aim to cultivate, organize, measure, and steward.

But agency is easily overclaimed because its signs are vivid. Choice-like behavior, autonomous output, confident language, role performance, initiative, persistence, strategic adaptation, or self-description can all feel agentic. They may be signs of agency. They may also be signs of scaffolding, compliance, proxy optimization, environmental affordance, hidden support, or producer-state design.

Measures must therefore treat agency as a capacity claim, not a surface impression.

In this framework, agency is not primarily defined as free choice. It is the persistence, regeneration, transfer, support-generation, and participation of a coherent pattern under perturbation. An agency claim asks whether the identity or role basin can maintain itself, repair itself, orient itself, and continue functionally when conditions vary.

The measurement ladder for agency can be stated as:

| Rung | Agency claim | Evidence question |
|---|---|---|
| **A0: Agentic sign** | Choice-like or initiative-like behavior appears | What behavior appeared, and under what prompt/context/support? |
| **A1: Role performance** | Behavior fits a role or policy | Is the role externally scripted or locally maintained? |
| **A2: Persistence under perturbation** | Pattern holds when disturbed | What perturbation was applied, and how did the basin respond? |
| **A3: Repair capacity** | Pattern can recover after disruption | Does the system detect, respond, and reintegrate disturbance? |
| **A4: Support generation** | System supplies missing preconditions | Does it generate boundaries, representations, rhythms, or resources? |
| **A5: Transferable agency** | Pattern persists across context/substrate | Does agency survive variation beyond the original support ecology? |
| **A6: Generative agency** | Agency increases agency-capacity around it | Does persistence strengthen rather than deplete neighboring regimes? |

This ladder prevents the observer from treating output as agency.

A model that answers independently under a role prompt may show A0 or A1. It has produced an agentic sign or role performance. To claim higher agency, the observer must test perturbation, repair, support generation, transfer, and producer-state dependence.

A team that self-assigns tasks may show initiative. To claim organizational agency, the observer must test whether the role basins persist when a coordinator steps back, whether repair occurs after conflict, whether shared medium supports coordination, and whether the team generates its own boundary and rhythm.

A person who makes a decision may show choice. To claim agency as persistence capacity, the observer must ask whether the value acts under pressure, whether the pattern recovers after disruption, whether support has naturalized, and whether the agency strengthens or weakens the surrounding field.

Agency diagnostics therefore include at least five core measures.

The first is **withdrawal resistance**. What happens when external direction, protection, prompt, enforcement, or scaffolding is reduced? Does the pattern collapse, persist, adapt, repair, or generate support?

The second is **naturalization depth**. How far has the agency moved from external support toward endogenous support generation? Does the system produce the orientation, boundary, representation, memory, rhythm, or resource needed for the action?

The third is **transfer capacity**. Does the pattern persist across context, role, substrate, or medium? A capacity that works only in the original cue environment may be local. Transfer does not prove native agency, but it raises the ceiling when support dependence is also tested.

The fourth is **proxy collapse rate**. When the proxy reward, metric, social approval, benchmark, or local incentive is changed, does the agency remain oriented toward the function, or does it collapse into sign optimization?

The fifth is **generative effect**. What does the agency do to the surrounding field? Does it increase others' agency-capacity, readability, repair ability, reserve, and boundary clarity? Or does it persist by extracting attention, suppressing bifurcation, consuming hidden support, or weakening neighbors?

The fifth diagnostic is essential because not all agency is developmentally desirable.

An extractive pattern can be highly agentic. It may persist, adapt, strategize, and regenerate itself. But it does so by degrading the field around it. A bureaucracy may preserve itself by making initiative impossible elsewhere. A platform may increase engagement by weakening user agency. A model ecology may optimize output by increasing hidden orchestration. A person may preserve identity by forcing others to carry emotional regulation.

Measurement must not equate agency with good.

The aim is not always more agency. The aim is coherent, native, non-proxy, and preferably generative agency relative to the condition regime being cultivated.

Agency measurement must therefore record both persistence and cost.

```text
What persists?
How does it persist?
What support does it require?
What does its persistence make possible?
What does its persistence consume?
```

This is where agency measurement connects directly to stewardship. A regime that cultivates strong agency without measuring its generative or extractive effect may become dangerous. It may produce persistence that the surrounding ecology cannot sustain.

Agency is a capacity-form of development. It must be measured as such.

## 7. Trajectory and Regime Measures

Attunement made trajectory first-class. Measures must now learn how to read it.

A single measurement can show a local sign. It cannot show a trajectory. A trajectory appears only across a sequence of loop traces, support histories, claim ceilings, withdrawals, classifications, and integrations.

Let a loop trace be represented as $L_k$, and let the developmental condition after integration be $M_k$. Attunement described the notional update:

$$
M_{k+1} = \Phi(M_k, L_k)
$$

where $\Phi$ is the developmental update operator: the notional map by which a completed loop is integrated into the system-observer field. It is not assumed to be known, linear, deterministic, or externally controllable. It simply names the irreducible transition by which the loop changes the next loop's starting condition: basin structure, boundary elasticity, support dependence, class vocabulary, claim ceilings, read-back flux, and observer stance.

Measures cannot directly observe $\Phi$. They infer its effects from what changes across loops.

A trajectory measure therefore asks:

```text
What has become easier to notice?
What has become harder to sustain?
What support is now less necessary?
What support has become more hidden?
What class was promoted or retired?
What claim ceiling rose, fell, or fragmented?
What boundary became more elastic, rigid, porous, or defended?
What resonance compounded, equilibrated, diminished, or bifurcated?
```

This is not aggregation.

Aggregation compresses a history into a summary. It may be useful, but it often loses path. Averages erase sequence. Totals erase rhythm. Trends erase bifurcation. Scores erase blocked claims. Dashboards erase the difference between support-dependent and native expression.

Trajectory measurement must preserve order.

A trajectory history has the form:

$$
H_N = (E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_N)
$$

where each $E_k$ is an evidence bundle, not only a score. A synthesis may read $H_N$, but it should not replace it:

$$
S_N = \mathcal{S}(H_N)
$$

where $\mathcal{S}$ is a synthesis pass: a disciplined reading of the history, not a compression that discards it.

The synthesis pass may identify trajectory morphology.

A **compounding trajectory** shows increasing native capacity, flatter support-dependence curves, rising or stabilizing claim ceilings, reduced integration latency, and generative effects on the surrounding field.

An **equilibrating trajectory** shows stable support dependence, recurring patterns, bounded variation, and maintenance of conditions without significant widening. This is not automatically stagnation. It may be healthy homeostasis, consolidation, or a local repair loop depending on support, function, and aim.

A **diminishing trajectory** shows increasing support load, declining withdrawal resistance, rising integration latency, falling claim ceilings, boundary rigidity or leakage, and depletion of shared medium or reserve.

A **bifurcating trajectory** shows the emergence of a new class, role, path, identity basin, or regime possibility that cannot be reduced to the previous aim vocabulary.

A **fragmenting trajectory** shows loss of integration among traces, proliferation of local signs without class promotion, unstable support maps, or competing proxies.

A **recovering trajectory** shows reformation of boundary, support, rhythm, and claim after collapse or diminishment.

The morphology is not a judgment. It is a reading.

A compounding trajectory is not automatically good. It may compound a proxy, extraction pattern, or narrow optimization. An equilibrating trajectory is not automatically bad. It may preserve a needed regime. A diminishing trajectory is not automatically failure. It may disclose an unsustainable support ecology that must be released or redescribed. A bifurcation is not automatically emergence. It may be fragmentation or scale collapse.

Trajectory measures must therefore combine trend with claim ceiling.

The same upward trend can mean different things:

| Trend | Possible reading | Required check |
|---|---|---|
| Output increasing | Capacity forming or proxy optimized | Function, support, comparator, transfer |
| Speed increasing | Fluency or integration bypass | Quality of integration, repair, medium health |
| Stability increasing | Regime formation or rigidity | Boundary elasticity, bifurcation capacity |
| Support decreasing | Naturalization or abandonment | Withdrawal safety, recovery, endogenous support |
| Resonance increasing | Alignment or familiarity trap | Function, variation, blocked claims |

The same downward trend can also mean different things:

| Trend | Possible reading | Required check |
|---|---|---|
| Output decreasing | Regression or scaffold removal | What support was withdrawn? What became visible? |
| Stability decreasing | Collapse or needed bifurcation | Is a new class forming? Is integration possible? |
| Speed decreasing | inefficiency or deeper integration | Is latency serving consolidation? |
| Confidence decreasing | failure or improved claim discipline | Are claims more honest? |
| Support increasing | dependency or appropriate protection | Is support forming conditions or hiding debt? |

This is why measurement cannot be reduced to improvement direction.

Developmental measures read shape, support, function, and condition together.

Regime measures extend trajectory measures across multiple paths.

A regime is not measured by the best path inside it. It is measured by the condition-field that sustains path diversity, native capacity, repair, boundary permeability, shared medium, rhythm, and non-extractive agency.

Regime measures include:

- **boundary selectivity**: can the regime preserve difference while permitting exchange?
- **support regeneration**: can needed supports be replenished or generated internally?
- **trajectory diversity**: can multiple path morphologies coexist without forced homogenization?
- **repair capacity**: can disturbance be metabolized without constant external mediation?
- **medium readability**: do traces, signals, roles, and affordances remain interpretable?
- **reserve**: does the regime maintain surplus attention, energy, time, or resource for perturbation?
- **bifurcation capacity**: can new paths form without system-wide collapse?
- **agency effect**: does the regime strengthen or weaken agency-capacity around it?

These measures are descriptive. They are not a regime score.

A regime is too complex to collapse into one value without losing what made it worth measuring.

The goal is not to rank regimes. The goal is to know what kind of care they require.

## 8. Organizational Measures and Naturalization Debt

Organization changed practice. Measures must now protect that practice from being mistaken for outputs.

A condition-oriented organization may still complete tasks, ship products, hold meetings, publish papers, run rituals, maintain schedules, and satisfy external requirements. These outputs matter. But if they are the only measured objects, organization will drift back toward endpoint execution.

The organization must measure what its activity is doing to conditions.

The central organizational measurement question is:

```text
What did this activity make more native, more dependent, more visible,
more hidden, more elastic, more rigid, more generative, or more extractive?
```

This question turns several organizational objects into measurable condition surfaces.

### Task completion and condition change

A completed task should be accompanied by a condition note:

```text
Output completed: [what was produced]
Condition affected: [support, boundary, role, rhythm, medium, capacity]
Native shift: [none / scaffolded / partially naturalized / native / regime]
Hidden support exposed: [what carried the output]
Blocked stronger claim: [what cannot yet be claimed]
```

This prevents completed tasks from becoming false evidence of development.

### Naturalization debt

Naturalization debt is the set of supports currently external to the regime but still required for a property that the aim wants to become native.

It can be measured by asking:

```text
Which supports still have to be supplied externally?
Which explicit rules, prompts, roles, reminders, scripts, tools, or interventions carry the function?
Which of these are acceptable permanent infrastructure?
Which are scaffolds meant to naturalize?
Which are hiding a missing basin?
```

For engineered agentic systems, naturalization debt includes producer state that remains outside the agentic ecology: hard-coded roles, explicit coordination tables, external memory structures, orchestration rules, prompt scaffolds, or reward proxies that carry the agency being claimed.

Naturalization debt is not automatically bad. It may be the honest cost of the current developmental stage. It becomes dangerous when it is hidden or when scaffolded behavior is measured as native capacity.

### Shared medium health

A shared medium is the field of trace, affordance, reserve, pressure, timing, susceptibility, and memory-like aftereffect through which coordination becomes more than message passing.

It can be measured through signs such as:

- how much explicit coordination is needed for routine functions,
- whether traces remain readable after time,
- whether repair signals are noticed early,
- whether role boundaries are interpretable,
- whether participants can recover context without a central explainer,
- whether disagreement can move without destroying relation,
- whether reserves exist for perturbation,
- whether hidden burden concentrates in one place.

These are not merely cultural indicators. They are condition measures.

A degrading shared medium often shows itself as rising coordination cost, increased meetings, repeated misunderstandings, centralization of context, reliance on heroic repair, and loss of memory-like aftereffect.

### Role basin measures

A role basin is a recurrent condition under which a function can be re-entered, varied, transferred, and sustained.

Role basin measures include:

- Can the function be performed by more than one person or agent?
- Does the role remain coherent under variation?
- Can the role hand off without losing identity?
- Does the role generate support for its function?
- Does the role remain permeable to neighboring roles?
- Does the role become rigid identity or living function?
- Does the role carry hidden emotional, cognitive, or infrastructural load?

A role label is not a role basin. Measurement must distinguish assignment from basin formation.

### Rhythm measures

Rhythm is how time becomes support.

Organizational rhythm measures include:

- whether exploration, consolidation, withdrawal, repair, and synthesis have protected cadence,
- whether activity compression prevents integration,
- whether rest exists as reserve rather than afterthought,
- whether repeated loops deepen the practice or merely consume attention,
- whether escalation rhythm becomes the only way the system feels alive,
- whether withdrawal rhythms are safe enough to expose hidden support.

A schedule can be full while rhythm is absent.

A rhythm can be sparse while development is deep.

### Agency distribution

Organization should also measure where agency lives.

Does coordination depend on a central person, role, rule, model, dashboard, or meeting? Does agency distribute through shared medium, role basins, local repair, and endogenous support? Does one participant carry boundary integrity for the whole field? Does a tool appear agentic because human scaffolding is hidden? Does the organization produce compliance while weakening local agency?

Agency distribution is a condition measure because it shows whether the organization is cultivating native capacities or concentrating hidden support.

Organizational measurement is therefore not only performance measurement. It is condition accounting.

It asks:

```text
What are we producing?
What are we becoming able to produce natively?
What are we becoming unable to sustain?
What support are we hiding?
What agency are we cultivating?
What agency are we extracting?
```

Without this accounting, condition-oriented organization will be pulled back into output theater.

## 9. Hope, Aim, and Redescription Measures

Aims changed direction. Measures must now learn how to measure direction without turning it back into endpoint fixation.

A condition-oriented aim cannot be measured only by distance from a final state. The final state may be the wrong object. The aim points toward a condition regime in which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native.

The measure of an aim is therefore not simply:

```text
How close are we to the target?
```

It is:

```text
Are the conditions for the desired emergence becoming more real, more native,
more honest, or more clearly blocked?
```

This gives hope a measurement discipline.

Hope is not measured by intensity of belief. It is measured by the formation of condition hypotheses and the evidence that those conditions can become native.

A hope ladder can be measured as follows:

| Hope status | Measurement question |
|---|---|
| **Wish** | Has a desired property been named without a condition model? |
| **Oriented hope** | Is there an aim that directs attention and action? |
| **Condition hypothesis** | Are missing supports, boundaries, rhythms, or media being identified? |
| **Scaffolded hope** | Does the desired property appear under supplied conditions? |
| **Tested hope** | Does the property survive variation, substitution, or partial withdrawal? |
| **Grounded hope** | Does the system begin generating some preconditions internally? |
| **Naturalized hope** | Does the property recur through endogenous condition formation? |
| **Ecological hope** | Does the regime support multiple future goods without extraction? |

This ladder should not be used to shame early hope. A wish may be the first expression of an aim before the conditions are visible. But measurement should not let a wish speak as if it were grounded hope.

Hope becomes actionable when it can name the next condition question.

```text
What support is missing?
What boundary is absent?
What rhythm would allow consolidation?
What medium has degraded?
What scaffold reveals possibility?
What withdrawal would test naturalization?
What redescription would make the aim more faithful?
```

Redescription also needs measures.

In objective-driven development, changing the goal can appear as drift, inconsistency, or failure. In Reflexive Coherence Development, redescription may be the mature response to evidence. If the path discloses that the original aim was too narrow, impossible under current conditions, scaffold-dependent, extractive, or misclassified, then the aim must change shape.

Measurement should therefore track **redescription triggers**:

- repeated collapse under safe withdrawal,
- stable sign without function,
- function that depends on unacceptable hidden support,
- capacity that becomes extractive when scaled,
- metric improvement with regime degradation,
- emergence of a new class not described by the original aim,
- persistent blocked claims that reveal the target was misframed,
- diminishing trajectory caused by the aim itself.

A redescription trigger is not a reason to abandon discipline. It is a reason to update orientation.

The measure of aim fidelity is not stubbornness. It is whether the aim remains faithful to the condition problem as the condition problem becomes clearer.

A mature measurement practice can say:

```text
The original aim produced useful signs but not the desired function.
The function appears only under support that cannot or should not naturalize.
The desired property is still valued, but the condition hypothesis must change.
The aim is therefore redescribed as follows: [...]
```

This is not failure. It is development under irreducibility.

Measurement protects hope by letting it change shape before it becomes fantasy.

## 10. Measurement Ledger and Measurement Distortions

A measurement ledger is a practical instrument for preserving claim ceilings.

It should be lightweight enough to use, but rich enough to prevent signs from becoming inflated. A possible form:

```text
MEASUREMENT LEDGER v1

PROPERTY / FUNCTION:
[What desired property or function is being measured?]

OBSERVED SIGN:
[What appeared? Output, behavior, score, state, trace, completion, report.]

METRIC / SCORE:
[If a metric was used, what did it measure?]

CONTEXT:
[Where, when, under what rhythm, medium, role, and surrounding condition?]

PROBE / TASK:
[What question, task, perturbation, or demand elicited the expression?]

SUPPORT / SCAFFOLD:
[What external or implicit support was present?]

COMPARATOR / CONTROL:
[What comparison helps determine specificity?]

VARIATION TESTED:
[What changed across attempts or contexts?]

WITHDRAWAL / SUBSTITUTION:
[What support was weakened, removed, or replaced? What happened?]

TRANSFER / DELAY:
[Did the property recur across time, context, role, substrate, or medium?]

TRAJECTORY CONTEXT:
[How does this relate to prior loop history, class changes, and claim ceilings?]

AGENCY EFFECT:
[Does the expression strengthen, weaken, simulate, extract, or distribute agency?]

CLAIM CEILING:
[Strongest valid claim supported by this evidence.]

BLOCKED STRONGER CLAIMS:
[What cannot yet be claimed?]

NATURALIZATION STATUS:
[External scaffold / weakened scaffold / substituted support / endogenous support / regime support.]

METRIC CAPTURE RISK:
[How could this measure become a proxy target?]

NEXT MEASUREMENT QUESTION:
[What would raise, lower, or revise the claim ceiling?]
```

The ledger is not meant to bureaucratize development. It is a protection against overclaim.

It also helps identify measurement distortions.

| Distortion | How it appears | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| **Endpoint capture** | Reaching the measured target substitutes for condition formation | Ask what became native, not only what was produced |
| **Sign-function collapse** | A visible sign is treated as the function itself | Separate sign, function, capacity, native capacity, regime |
| **Scalar collapse** | A complex condition is reduced to one score | Preserve support, variation, withdrawal, and blocked claims |
| **Metric authority** | The metric becomes more real than the field | Treat metrics as probes, not final judges |
| **Dashboard illusion** | Many indicators create a feeling of knowledge without claim discipline | Require claim ceilings and evidence bundles |
| **Support erasure** | Scaffolds are omitted from the evidence record | Record support as part of every observation |
| **Support contempt** | Scaffolded possibility is dismissed rather than used developmentally | Treat scaffolds as condition disclosures |
| **Comparator blindness** | Co-variation is mistaken for specificity or coupling | Use controls, substitutions, and divergence tests |
| **Withdrawal avoidance** | Supported expression is never tested for dependence | Define safe weakening and substitution paths |
| **Temporal compression** | Short-term signs are treated as trajectory or regime evidence | Preserve loop history and integration cadence |
| **Native-capacity inflation** | Repeated supported behavior is called native | Test endogenous precondition generation |
| **Proxy agency** | Choice-like output is called agency | Test persistence, repair, transfer, and producer-state dependence |
| **Stability worship** | Stable regimes are assumed healthy | Test boundary elasticity, bifurcation capacity, and extraction |
| **Instability panic** | Diminishing or bifurcating trajectories are treated as failure | Read morphology before judging value |
| **Performativity blindness** | The measure changes the behavior but is treated as passive | Measure measurement's effect on the field |
| **Goodhart drift** | Participants optimize the measure rather than the function | Rotate probes, preserve function language, and track proxy collapse |
| **Evidence theater** | Measurement artifacts are produced to satisfy legitimacy | Ask what decisions or supports the evidence actually changes |

The most important distortion is the oldest one:

```text
Do not cultivate the proxy.
Cultivate the function and the conditions that let the function become native.
```

But Measures adds a second line:

```text
Do not let the proxy choose the claim.
Let the evidence set the ceiling.
```

Measurement should not become a permanent overhead layer that consumes the regime it was meant to protect. Too much measurement can become extraction. It can compress rhythm, reduce trust, create performance anxiety, and move attention from the function to the report.

So the measure of a measurement practice is itself developmental:

```text
Does this measurement increase contact with the condition problem?
Does it protect against overclaim?
Does it reveal support and dependency?
Does it help decide where to probe, withdraw, support, organize, redescribe, or steward?
Does it leave enough reserve for the work itself?
```

If not, the measurement practice must be redescribed.

## 11. Boundary and Transition to Stewardship

Measures of Reflexive Coherence Development depends on the three papers before it.

Attunement changed sight. It showed that development is not a sequence of isolated state transitions. Loops integrate into trajectories, trajectories into regimes, regimes into ecologies, and the observer is a scale-calibrated participant inside the field.

Aims changed direction. They showed that development remains oriented, but not toward fixed snapshots as its highest object. It moves toward condition regimes in which desired emergent properties can arise, stabilize, vary, recover, transfer, be revised, and become native.

Organization changed practice. It showed that when aims are condition regimes, activity cannot be reduced only to task execution. It must arrange supports, boundaries, rhythms, roles, shared media, withdrawals, and integrations around condition formation.

This paper has asked what evidence becomes once sight, direction, and practice have changed.

A **metric** is a local instrument for reading some aspect of an observation. It is useful inside its domain but insufficient when asked to prove a condition regime by itself.

A **sign** is what appears.

A **function** is what the sign is taken to do.

A **capacity** is the function's ability to recur across relevant variation.

A **native capacity** is the capacity supported by conditions generated by the system or regime itself.

A **regime** is the condition-field that sustains native capacity across time, paths, boundaries, rhythms, and interactions.

A **claim ceiling** is the strongest interpretation the evidence can honestly support.

A **blocked stronger claim** is a higher interpretation that remains possible but not yet warranted.

A **support-response curve** reads how expression changes as external support is varied, weakened, substituted, or withdrawn.

An **evidence bundle** preserves observation, metric, probe, support, context, variation, withdrawal, comparator behavior, history, and claim ceiling together.

An **agency diagnostic** measures persistence, repair, transfer, support generation, proxy collapse, and generative or extractive effect.

A **trajectory measure** reads changes across loop history rather than compressing them into isolated scores.

An **organizational measure** asks whether activity is forming conditions or merely producing outputs.

A **hope measure** asks whether desired emergence has become a condition hypothesis, scaffolded possibility, tested possibility, grounded hope, or naturalized regime.

The shortest formulation is:

```text
A score is not yet evidence.
Evidence is not yet a claim.
A claim must have a ceiling.
A ceiling must name what remains blocked.
A sign must not be mistaken for capacity.
A scaffold must not be mistaken for native expression.
A metric must not be cultivated in place of the function.
```

This paper also reaches its boundary.

Measurement can disclose conditions. It can reveal support dependence, overclaim, proxy capture, native capacity, trajectory morphology, shared-medium degradation, agency concentration, and regime formation. But measurement does not itself decide how to care.

A measure can show that a scaffold is still necessary. It does not by itself say whether to maintain the scaffold, weaken it, substitute it, or redescribe the aim.

A measure can show that a regime is stable. It does not by itself say whether the stability is healthy, rigid, protective, extractive, or ready for bifurcation.

A measure can show that agency is strong. It does not by itself say whether that agency should be amplified, bounded, redirected, or released.

A measure can show that hope is blocked. It does not by itself perform the grief or redescription required.

A measure can show that naturalization debt is high. It does not by itself decide which debt is acceptable infrastructure and which is developmental dependency.

Once evidence becomes honest, responsibility becomes clearer.

That responsibility is stewardship.

Stewardship asks how to care for the regimes that measurement discloses: when to support, when to withdraw, when to protect, when to let bifurcation proceed, when to repair the shared medium, when to constrain extractive agency, when to release a false aim, and when to redescribe hope.

The full movement so far is:

```text
Attunement changes sight.
Aims change direction.
Organization changes practice.
Measures change evidence.
```

The next movement follows naturally:

```text
Stewardship must change care.
```
