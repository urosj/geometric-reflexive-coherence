# Basin Templates

These templates provide a lightweight human-facing form of the basin workflow.
They are adapted from lessons in the companion
[reflexive-coherence-agentic-protocol](https://github.com/urosj/reflexive-coherence-agentic-protocol)
project, but they avoid full protocol ceremony.

Use them to preserve returnability:

1. copy the relevant template into a basin's `artifact-space/`;
2. rename it using `<aspect>-<subject-slug>-<actor-slug>.md`;
3. keep the same subject slug across related artifacts;
4. fill only the fields that matter for the contribution;
5. update the basin's `artifact-space/index.md`.

For small contributions, brief answers are fine. The template exists to keep
the chain recoverable, not to force long documents.

## Artifact Templates

- `proposition.template.md`
- `implementation.template.md`
- `validation.template.md`
- `interpretation.template.md`
- `promotion.template.md`

## Refinement Template

- `refinement-basin-readme.template.md`

Refinement is represented as a child basin, not as a normal parent-basin
artifact.
