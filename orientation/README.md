# Orientation

This directory contains program-level introductions to Reflexive Coherence.

The papers do not form another technical theory sequence. They provide
different entrances into the wider RC program: its motivating question, its
relevance across research domains, and the common direction connecting the
theory, substrates, experiments, developmental work, and agentic ecology.

## Reading Paths

### New To Reflexive Coherence

Start with:

1. [What Can Become?](2026-07-WhatCanBecome.md)
   A short, example-led introduction to the shift from selecting among
   available possibilities to participating in their formation.

### Entering From Another Research Field

Read:

2. [Why Reflexive Coherence? Eight Research Entry Points](2026-07-WhyReflexiveCoherence-EightResearchEntryPoints.md)
   Positions the work relative to autonomous agents, ecology, complex systems,
   artificial life, embodied cognition, control, process ontology, and
   scientific methodology.

### Looking For The Full Program Argument

Continue with:

3. [The Formation of Possibility](2026-07-TheFormationOfPossibility.md)
   A program-level orientation to endogenous possibility formation, becoming,
   aims, agency, shared worlds, evidence discipline, and the relationship among
   the RC projects.
