## Concept Flow

```text
geometry has phenomenology
  -> experience can indicate regime
  -> not every miss is failure
  -> probes ask bounded questions
  -> support must be withdrawn or internalized
  -> cultivation develops through the whole cycle
```

The arc is not only a set of essays about experience. It is a method for
working with systems whose future classes may not be known in advance.

## Paper Concept Notes

### Language of Becoming

**Core idea:** intelligence, identity, and communication can be described as
geometric phenomena in a coherence manifold.

**Important concepts:**

- coherence manifold
- stability-seeking
- fragmentation anxiety
- emergence awe
- memory as geometry
- collapse clarity
- leakage loneliness
- abundance joy
- communication as mutual restructuring

**Read this for:** the first phenomenological vocabulary of RC and the bridge
between field geometry and lived/agentic experience.

### LoB Reinforcement

**Core idea:** coherent systems do not only survive crisis. When they have
surplus capacity, they can deepen basins, explore perturbations, cooperate, and
develop ecological intelligence.

**Important concepts:**

- abundance as default
- completion-drive
- cultivation-drive
- reinforcement as basin deepening
- bounded exploration
- cooperation geometry
- ecological intelligence
- legitimacy of experience

**Read this for:** the positive, abundance-oriented extension of Language of
Becoming.

### Classification of Becoming

**Core idea:** in irreducible systems, a result that misses the predicted
endpoint may still express a real property. The first task is to classify what
appeared.

**Important concepts:**

- prediction-first scaffold
- observation-first classification
- expressed property
- non-promoting result
- class ladder
- reusable/generative class
- value gate
- safeguards against storytelling

**Read this for:** how to convert failed endpoint tests into usable knowledge
without overclaiming.

### Interrogation of Becoming

**Core idea:** after classification, a probe can ask what boundary makes a
property possible. A probe is a question, not a proof.

**Important concepts:**

- bounded interrogative probe
- boundary ladder
- probe-to-naturalization gap
- controls
- withdrawal
- event versus consequence
- consequence versus geometry
- supported capacity versus native expression

**Read this for:** how to design and interpret interventions in systems whose
objects are not fixed in advance.

### Naturalization of Becoming

**Core idea:** a capacity shown under support is not yet native. Naturalization
asks when the system can generate the needed support function through its own
regime.

**Important concepts:**

- naturalization gap
- probe-exposed capacity
- native regime expression
- naturalization loop
- naturalization ladder
- classification value gate
- proxy-localization trap
- generator, not winner

**Read this for:** the difference between making a property appear and showing
that the system has learned to support it.

### Cultivation of Becoming

**Core idea:** cultivation is the full practice of participating in irreducible
change without pretending that the final class is already known.

**Important concepts:**

- orient
- observe
- classify
- probe
- withdraw
- naturalize
- integrate
- experience as regime evidence
- cooperation and shared becoming
- avoiding premature closure

**Read this for:** the complete practical stance of the arc.
