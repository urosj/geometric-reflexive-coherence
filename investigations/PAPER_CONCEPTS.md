## Concept Flow

```text
coherence support
  -> induced metric
  -> geodesic distance
  -> operational travel time
  -> graph distance
  -> typed experience witnesses
```

The distance papers make the geometry precise. RCL then asks how expressed
patterns in that geometry can be named, typed, recognized, and composed.
