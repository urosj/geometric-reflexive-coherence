# Investigations

The `investigations/` directory contains focused research notes that refine
specific parts of Geometric Reflexive Coherence. These are not the main theory
sequence; they are supporting constructions that make particular ideas more
precise.

The current investigations focus on two themes:

```text
support-derived space and distance
typed experience and operational language
```

## Paper Summaries

### [RCL: A Minimal Calculus for Typed Experience](2025-12-22-RCL-iteration3.md)

This investigation develops a Reflexive Coherence Language (RCL): a minimal
calculus for typed experience. It introduces pattern witnesses, interfaces,
composition expressions, semantic equivalence, primitive experience types
(`identity`, `spark`, `split`), and operational realizability through GRC-v2.

Its main contribution is a bridge from RC phenomenology to implementation:
experience labels are not just informal names, but can be tied to witnesses,
interfaces, recognizers, supports, and graph traces.

### [RC Geometry: Induced Distance and Support-Derived Space](2026-01-RC-Distance-v4.md)

This investigation gives the rigorous distance construction for RC. It defines
space as the support of coherence, constructs a positive-definite induced
metric from the coherence tensor, distinguishes geodesic distance from
operational travel-time distance, and bridges continuum distance to graph
distance.

Its main contribution is the claim that RC does not place organisms into a
fixed background space. Space, geometry, distance, refinement, and motion are
derived from the coherence field and its induced metric.

### [Distance and Spacetime in Reflexive Coherence](2026-01-RC-Distance-Spacetime.md)

This investigation develops an earlier or companion narrative around distance,
proper time, hierarchical distance, anisotropic shortcuts from flux, and
spacetime growth as refinement. It also connects the distance story to FRC by
showing how scale-expanding hierarchies extend the picture.

Its main contribution is an accessible conceptual bridge: distance, proper
time, and growth are not independent primitives, but consequences of coherence,
support, flux, and basin hierarchy.

## Conceptual Progression

The investigations can be read as support material for public readers who ask:

- How can RC define space without assuming a fixed container?
- What kind of distance is being measured?
- Why are geodesic, operational, and graph distance different?
- How can experience labels become operationally recognizable?
- How can GRC traces instantiate typed RC phenomena?
