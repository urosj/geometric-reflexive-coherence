## Concept Flow

```text
structural abundance
  -> embedded uncertainty
  -> persistence through perturbation
  -> agency after choice
  -> read-back and interiority
  -> sentience as registered flow
```

The essays form a short interpretive arc. Abundance shows that RC does not end
in uniformity. Agency asks what can hold through differentiation. Sentience
asks when that holding is internally registered as experience.

## Essay Concept Notes

### From Structural Abundance To Agency

**Core idea:** RC already contains structural abundance, but abundance does not
yet explain agency. It creates the conditions under which agency becomes the
next question.

**Important concepts:**

- structural abundance
- basin proliferation
- differentiated identity geometry
- embedded observer
- novelty before classification
- surplus capacity
- coupling and coherence cost
- Arc of Becoming discipline

**Read this for:** the bridge from the formal abundance claim to the agency
problem.

### Agency Of Becoming

**Core idea:** agency is not the moment of choice. Under an RC/FRC reading,
agency is the persistence capacity of an identity basin before and after
collapse.

**Important concepts:**

- RC-agency
- identity basin
- collapse as local choice event
- FRC attractor tube
- withdrawal resistance
- naturalization depth
- substrate transfer capacity
- proxy collapse rate
- generative agency

**Read this for:** the main RC-centered agency interpretation and its proposed
diagnostics.

### Agency After Choice

**Core idea:** if agency means persistence through uncertainty, many patterns
become visible that choice-centered language misrecognizes or ignores.

**Important concepts:**

- scaffolded agency
- native agency
- proxy agency
- latent agency
- wounded agency
- extractive agency
- generative agency
- agency-capacity of environments

**Read this for:** the broader vocabulary for recognizing agency in persons,
institutions, cultures, swarms, ecosystems, and other persistent patterns.

### Sentience As Read-Back

**Core idea:** agency and sentience are related but not identical. Sentience
begins when a system's own coherence flow is read back into the geometry that
shapes its next flow.

**Important concepts:**

- read-back flux
- interior registration
- temporal integration
- present curvature
- flow as experience
- proxy sentience
- performed sentience
- native sentience
- scaling sentience

**Read this for:** the distinction between persistence, feedback, memory,
interiority, and conscious reflection.
