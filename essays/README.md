# Essays

The `essays/` directory contains interpretive extensions of Geometric
Reflexive Coherence. These papers are less foundational than the `core/`
sequence and less methodological than the `arc-of-becoming/` sequence. They
use RC, FRC, and the Arc of Becoming to develop vocabulary around abundance,
agency, persistence, sentience, and read-back.

Read together, the essays answer one question:

```text
What becomes visible when RC is read from the inside of becoming:
abundance as differentiation, agency as persistence, cultivation as discipline,
and sentience as read-back?
```

## Essay Summaries

### [From Structural Abundance to Agency](2026-06-12-FromStructuralAbundanceToAgency.md)

This essay bridges the structural abundance claim in RC with the question of
agency. It distinguishes abundance as basin proliferation under conserved
coherence from abundance as encountered by an embedded observer: novelty,
uncertainty, pressure, cost, and unclassified possibility.

Its main contribution is the reframing of abundance from a structural tendency
into the question of persistence: if coherence differentiates, collapses, and
renews, what kind of pattern can hold through becoming?

### [Agency of Becoming: An Interpretation Through Reflexive Coherence](2026-06-12-AgencyOfBecoming-InterpretationThroughRC.md)

This paper gives the RC-centered agency interpretation. It relocates choice to
collapse events and treats agency as the persistence capacity of an identity
basin across perturbation, withdrawal, substrate change, and proxy pressure.

Its main contribution is a disciplined RC account of agency as persistence
capacity rather than choice. It proposes withdrawal resistance, naturalization
depth, substrate transfer capacity, and proxy collapse rate as diagnostics,
then distinguishes first-order agency from generative agency.

### [Agency After Choice](2026-06-12-AgencyAfterChoice.md)

This essay asks what follows if agency-as-persistence is treated as a more
general vocabulary. It applies agency beyond RC to persons, institutions,
cultures, agentic swarms, ecosystems, and cosmological becoming.

Its main contribution is recognitional: it names forms of agency hidden by
choice-centered language, including scaffolded agency, native agency, proxy
agency, latent agency, wounded agency, extractive agency, and generative
agency.

### [Sentience as Read-Back](2026-06-12-SentienceAsReadBack.md)

This essay separates agency from sentience. Agency is persistence through
uncertainty; sentience begins when a reflexively closed system internally
registers its own coherence flow as read-back into present curvature.

Its main contribution is an RC-native account of sentience as integrated
read-back. It distinguishes read-back from memory, feedback from interiority,
performed sentience from native sentience, and agency from conscious
reflection.
